Budget watch · Updated June 28, 2026

Stay NJ is set to change with the new state budget

New Jersey has to pass its next state budget by June 30, with the new budget year starting July 1. That budget decides how Stay NJ works going forward. In late June, the Governor and legislative leaders announced a deal that keeps Stay NJ but tightens who qualifies. For now it is an agreement, not final law, and the official details are still being worked out.

Current law (Stay NJ as written)

Cuts an eligible senior's property tax bill in half, up to $6,500.
Open to homeowners 65 and older with income up to $500,000.

Reported budget deal (not final)

Top benefit stays at $6,500.
Income limit drops from $500,000 to $200,000, with the benefit tiered by income:
  • Income $100,000 or lessup to $6,500
  • $100,000 to $150,000up to $5,000
  • $150,000 to $200,000up to $4,000
  • Over $200,000not eligible
If your household income is over $200,000, you would no longer qualify under the reported deal. These figures come from news reports as of June 28, 2026 and are not yet written into law. We will update this page with the official numbers once the budget is signed. The Senior Freeze program is not affected by these proposed changes.

The main relief programs

Most NJ residents qualify for at least one of these. You have to apply to get them.

ANCHOR

A direct payment for NJ homeowners and renters, with no age requirement. Up to $1,500 for homeowners, up to $700 for renters.

Learn more →

Stay NJ · changing, see above

For homeowners 65 and older. Cuts an eligible senior's property tax bill in half, up to $6,500.

Learn more →

Senior Freeze (PTR)

For seniors and disabled homeowners. Reimburses the increase in your property taxes above your base year.

Learn more →

Veterans benefits

A $250 deduction for eligible NJ veterans, up to a full property tax exemption for 100% disabled veterans.
