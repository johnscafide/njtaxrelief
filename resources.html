Terminology

NJ Property Tax Glossary

Clear definitions for complex local tax programs.

ANCHOR Program
Affordable New Jersey Communities for Homeowners and Renters. A property tax relief program that replaced the Homestead Benefit, providing credits to eligible NJ residents.
Base Year
The specific tax year used to determine the "frozen" tax amount for the Senior Freeze (PTR) program. This is typically the year you first became eligible.
Stay NJ
A new tax credit program (starting 2026) designed to cut property taxes in half for many New Jersey seniors, capped at $6,500.
Senior Freeze (PTR)
Formally known as the Property Tax Reimbursement. It reimburses eligible senior citizens and disabled persons for property tax increases on their principal residence.
Abatement (Residential)
A temporary reduction or elimination of property taxes, often granted for 5 years following specific home improvements in designated New Jersey areas.
Questions & Answers

Commonly Asked Questions

Can I receive both ANCHOR and Senior Freeze?

Yes. Eligible residents can receive both the ANCHOR benefit and the Senior Freeze (PTR) reimbursement. They are separate programs with different applications.

What is the income limit for the 2026 ANCHOR program?

For homeowners, the income limit is generally $250,000. For renters, the income limit is $150,000. These figures are based on the specific tax year being filed.

Do I need to file every year?

Yes. Both the ANCHOR and Senior Freeze programs require annual filings to confirm your continued eligibility and current income status.
