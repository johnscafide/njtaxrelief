Most CRMs use the same handoff pattern.Upload CSV → map headers → review duplicates → choose automation → import.

  1. 1
    Export a clean standard CSV.

    Keep the Watchdog copy in your library as a dated backup of what you moved.

  2. 2
    Find Import Contacts in your CRM.

    Look under Contacts, People, Database, Leads, Settings or an Import / Migration area.

  3. 3
    Map each Watchdog heading.

    Unmapped fields are usually skipped. Pay special attention to email, phone, source, tags and notes.

  4. 4
    Choose duplicate behavior deliberately.

    If the CRM asks whether to skip, merge or overwrite existing contacts, test a few known records first.

  5. 5
    Confirm consent and automations.

    A contact being present in your database is not automatically permission for every marketing channel. Follow your brokerage policy and applicable law.
