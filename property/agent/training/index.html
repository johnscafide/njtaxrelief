LESSON 1 OF 8

Start with the Watchdog mindset.

Watchdog is a New Jersey property-intelligence layer built to help you research faster, explain property context clearly, and move the result into the next real estate workflow.

Core training
Think “evidence companion,” not “another MLS.”

Use your MLS for inventory, showing activity, listing history and pricing. Use Watchdog to add tax, assessment, municipal, permit, recorded-property and other sourced context around the property.

Research

Start with an address and bring fragmented public-property information into one working view.

Explain

Keep the source, date and limitation attached so you know what you can safely tell a client.

Act

Move from research into a buyer, listing, contact, monitoring or transaction workflow without rebuilding the file.

Three rules every agent should know

  1. Watchdog does not replace title work, attorney review, inspections, the MLS or a municipal clearance. It helps you identify what to investigate and preserves the evidence you found.
  2. “No issue observed” is not the same as “no issue exists.” Coverage, provider availability and source freshness matter.
  3. Property data is not seller intent. A permit, deed or assessment change can be a useful reason to review a property; it is not proof someone wants to sell.
Once you understand the evidence boundary, the rest of Watchdog makes much more sense.

LESSON 5 OF 8

Clean your sphere before you automate it.

Watchdog’s Contact Cleanup is designed to make a messy export usable while preserving the original and keeping your CRM as the destination system.

Included with Agent
Upload & archive

Import a CSV and keep the source file attached to your private Watchdog account.

Review & clean

Normalize common fields, inspect duplicates and fix obvious contact-data problems before handoff.

Export deliberately

Create a BoldTrail-ready CSV or a clean universal CSV for another system.

What Watchdog does not claim today

The current free Agent workflow prepares the file; it does not claim to push records directly into BoldTrail. The separate BoldTrail Direct beta is read-only today.

Planned

More CRM presets and deeper integrations. The export layer is designed so additional CRM mappings can be added without changing how you maintain the original contact file.
