Refunds and cancellations

Watchdog subscriptions provide immediate access to digital property intelligence, research tools and plan-specific features. This policy explains when refunds may be available and how to request one.

Effective August 10, 2026 · Commercial policy — subject to applicable law and the checkout terms presented by the active payment provider

Subscription cancellations

You may cancel a recurring Watchdog subscription at any time through the supported billing portal. Unless otherwise stated at checkout, cancellation stops future renewals and your paid access remains available through the end of the current paid billing period.

Refund requests

If you believe a charge was made in error, you experienced a material billing issue, or the paid service was not provided as described, contact us promptly. Refund requests are reviewed individually and may be approved when appropriate based on the circumstances, applicable law, and the transaction status.

Renewal charges

Recurring subscriptions renew automatically on the billing cadence shown at checkout unless canceled before renewal. Refunds are not guaranteed for renewal charges simply because the subscription was not canceled before the renewal date, but we will review reasonable requests submitted promptly after a renewal.

Annual plans

Annual paid plans, when offered, are billed in advance for the annual term. If an annual subscription is canceled, access generally continues through the end of the paid term. Any approved refund may be full or partial depending on the reason for the request, usage, timing, and applicable consumer-protection requirements.

Teams plans

Teams subscriptions are billed on the cadence presented at enrollment or in the applicable written agreement. Refund eligibility for a Teams account may also be governed by a separate organization or enterprise agreement.

Duplicate, unauthorized or incorrect charges

If you see a duplicate charge, an incorrect amount, or a payment you do not recognize, contact us as soon as possible so the transaction can be investigated. Do not share full payment-card numbers or other sensitive payment credentials when contacting support.

How to request a refund

Send your request to hello@njpropertytaxrelief.com and include the email address associated with your Watchdog account, the approximate charge date, the plan purchased, and a short explanation of the issue. We may request additional information needed to locate the transaction.

Payment processing

Watchdog uses a third-party payment provider for supported subscription payments. The provider in use is identified during checkout and account management. Approved refunds are returned through the original payment method when supported by that provider and the applicable payment network. Processing times after approval may vary by bank, card issuer or payment method.

Consumer rights

Nothing in this policy limits any non-waivable refund, cancellation or consumer rights that apply under applicable law. If a law gives you greater rights than this policy, those legal rights control.
