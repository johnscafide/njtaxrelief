New Jersey professional play

Find NJ appeal prospects by dollars at stake, not just percentage

Screen a New Jersey farm for Chapter 123 assessment exposure and prioritize properties where the estimated annual dollars justify a closer review.

Who this is for

Tax appeal attorneys, certified tax representatives, agents and investors screening a municipality or farm.

The question it answers

Which properties appear above the Chapter 123 corridor, and where is the estimated annual tax exposure large enough to warrant evidence review?

What public data it runs on

Primary sources: NJ MOD-IV assessment records, NJ Division of Taxation SR-1A verified sales, and published common-level / Chapter 123 ratio data.

Worked New Jersey example

Cherry Hill example: If a property is assessed at $500,000 and independent verified-sale evidence supports $400,000 of market value, its assessment-to-value ratio is 1.25. If the applicable upper Chapter 123 limit is 1.10, the property screens above the corridor. Watchdog then estimates the assessment reduction and annual tax dollars at stake so the largest opportunities rise first.

What it does not tell you

This is a screening workflow, not legal advice and not a prediction that a county board will grant a reduction. Comparable selection, filing deadlines, usable sales and property-specific facts still control the case.
