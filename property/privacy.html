Privacy at Watchdog

We collect the minimum account, product and billing information needed to operate Watchdog. Public property data and customer account data are treated as separate systems.

Effective August 5, 2026 · Product policy draft for pre-launch review

Information we process

Account identifiers supplied through authentication; saved properties, reports and Data Center configurations; professional role and plan information; product usage and diagnostic events; support requests; and billing identifiers/status supplied by Stripe. Watchdog does not need or receive your full card number from Stripe.

Public property information

Watchdog organizes public-government property, assessment, tax, sale, permit, municipal-finance and environmental records. Public-source data is not treated as a customer profile. Owner names are not reconstructed or restored when they are redacted from the public source.

Service providers

We use service providers for authentication/database hosting, subscription billing, email delivery, website analytics and infrastructure. They receive information only as needed to provide those functions and are subject to their own privacy terms.

Retention and account requests

Operational records are retained as needed for the service, security, billing, legal compliance and dispute resolution. Account-access, correction or deletion requests can be submitted through the site contact channel. Some billing, security and audit records may need to be retained after account deletion.

Not a consumer-reporting service

Watchdog is not designed or offered as a consumer reporting agency. It may not be used for eligibility decisions involving credit, employment, tenant screening, insurance eligibility, or another regulated consumer-reporting purpose.
