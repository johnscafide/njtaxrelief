3. Financial profile information and consent

Some optional profile fields are personal financial details, including income, mortgage balance and terms, escrow and credit range. We treat those fields with extra care, but we do not describe every financial fact as statutory sensitive data under the New Jersey Data Privacy Act. The Act's sensitive financial-information category is narrower and includes financial account or access information that, together with required security or access credentials, could permit access to a consumer's financial account.

Watchdog does not ask for bank account numbers, card numbers, financial account passwords, security codes, Social Security numbers or government ID numbers. The ordinary profile figures we do ask about are still personal data when linked to your account, so they are optional and blank by default. Saving them authorizes us to use them only for the purposes explained in section 4. You can clear them later or ask us to delete them.

We use a data-protection assessment process for higher-risk personal-data uses and review new purposes, profiling and material connectors before expanding how account data is used. We also keep these profile fields separate from public property records and do not make them public or searchable.

Some benefit programs depend on income, age, household or other eligibility thresholds that can change over time. We ask for the information needed to answer the question you requested rather than treating the profile as a requirement to use the rest of the site.

4. Why we collect it

  • To answer the question you asked. Estimating a benefit or screening a tax appeal needs your age, household and income, because those are what the statutes turn on.
  • To keep track of your properties and show you how their assessments move over time.
  • To respond to you when you ask for a valuation, a proposal, or an appeal review.
  • To send you the alerts you chose, such as filing deadlines or a revaluation in your town. You control these in your profile.
  • To improve the site, using aggregate patterns rather than individual behavior.
  • To meet legal and professional obligations, including real estate licensing recordkeeping.

We do not use your profile to make automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects about you. The estimates on this site are informational, and a person reviews anything that turns into real advice.

5. Public property records

Most of what this site displays is not personal information at all. Assessments, tax bills, block and lot numbers, lot lines and recorded sales are public records published by the New Jersey Office of GIS and the Division of Taxation. They describe properties, not people.

We built it that way deliberately. Owner names are redacted from the public data under Daniel's Law, and we do not add them back. This site cannot be searched by a person's name and is not designed to identify who lives anywhere. We have no plans to change that.

When you look up an address, we record the property's public figures in an anonymous ledger so the site gets faster and can show assessment history over time. That record contains no information about you, not your identity, your IP address, or your session.

Ownership verification is the one place where a person is linked to a property, and only because you asked us to confirm it. That link stays inside your account. It is never published, never sold, and never made searchable.

7. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information, and never have.
  • We do not share it with data brokers or people search sites.
  • We do not use it for targeted advertising or build advertising profiles.
  • We do not publish owner names, or let this site be searched by a person's name.
  • We do not sell or share the contact details of the professionals you list in your profile.
  • We do not text or call you unless you asked us to.

Advertising on this site is fixed placement. Advertisers pay to appear; they do not receive your information and cannot target you through us.

8. Cookies and tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies for three things: keeping you signed in, remembering your preferences, and measuring traffic. There are no advertising cookies on this site.

When a visit arrives with campaign information such as UTM parameters or a click identifier, we may remember the first and most recent campaign that brought the browser here. If you later submit a form, that campaign context can be attached to the submission so we can understand which outreach produced the inquiry. We use it for our own lead-source measurement, not to build or sell an advertising profile.

Microsoft Clarity records anonymized page sessions so we can see where the site is confusing. Clarity masks text entered into form fields by default, so your income and mortgage figures are not captured in those recordings.

Global Privacy Control. If your browser sends a GPC or similar universal opt out signal, we treat it as a valid request to opt out of any sale or targeted advertising, and we honor it automatically. You can also disable analytics through your browser settings or an extension without losing any functionality here.

9. Your rights

If you live in New Jersey, the New Jersey Data Privacy Act gives you the right to:

  • Know what personal data we hold about you
  • Correct anything inaccurate
  • Delete your data
  • Take it with you in a portable, machine readable format
  • Opt out of any sale, targeted advertising, or profiling
  • Withdraw consent for sensitive data at any time

We extend these same rights to every visitor regardless of where you live, because drawing a line based on your address would be a strange thing to do.

Most of it you can do yourself. Your profile is editable at any time, saved properties can be removed with one click, and signing out ends the session. For anything else, submit a request through Watchdog Privacy Support and identify it as a privacy request. We respond within 45 days, and will tell you if we need longer, which the law allows in limited cases.

We will not charge you for a request, deny you service for making one, or give you a worse experience because of it. If we refuse a request we will explain why, and you may appeal by replying to that message. If you are still unsatisfied you can contact the New Jersey Division of Consumer Affairs.

10. How long we keep it

  • Account and profile: until you delete your account, or after three years of inactivity, whichever comes first
  • Saved properties: until you remove them or close your account
  • Form submissions and correspondence: up to three years, or longer where real estate recordkeeping rules require it
  • Verification codes: 30 days, then they expire
  • Analytics: according to each provider's default retention, typically 14 to 26 months
  • The anonymous property ledger: kept indefinitely, because it contains only public property figures and no information about any person

11. Security

Accounts are protected by row level security, which means the database itself enforces that you can only ever read or write your own rows. That is not a setting in our code that could be bypassed; it is a rule inside the database. Connections use HTTPS. Sign in is handled by Supabase Auth, and we never see or store a password.

No system is perfectly secure, and anyone who tells you otherwise is selling something. If we ever have a breach affecting your personal information, we will notify you and the appropriate authorities as the law requires.

12. Children and teens

This site is meant for adults and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13. If we learn we have, we delete it.

For anyone we know to be between 13 and 17, we will not sell their data, use it for targeted advertising, or profile them, which New Jersey law requires consent for and we simply do not do at all.

13. Not a consumer report

This is important, so it gets its own section. Nothing on this site is a consumer report under the Fair Credit Reporting Act, and none of it may be used to make decisions about a person.

Specifically, you may not use this site or anything exported from it for tenant screening, employment screening, credit decisions, insurance underwriting, or any similar purpose. We are not a consumer reporting agency. The data here describes properties, not people, and using it to judge a person is both a violation of our terms and potentially of federal law.

The estimates, valuations and appeal screenings on this site are informational. They are not appraisals, not legal advice, and not tax advice. Confirm anything important with your municipal assessor, a licensed appraiser, an attorney, or your accountant before acting on it.

15. Things not built yet

This site is actively being developed. Rather than quietly expanding what we collect later, here is what may be added, and the commitment attached to each:

  • Document uploads, such as a tax bill for verification. Stored privately, visible only to your account, deletable by you.
  • Text message alerts. Opt in only, with a clear way to stop.
  • MLS or IDX listing data. Would be governed by MLS rules in addition to this policy.
  • Profile photo uploads. Stored privately, not public.
  • Data exports and reports containing your own saved information.

Two things will not change. We will not start selling personal information, and we will not make this site searchable by a person's name. If either ever became necessary, we would notify you in advance and ask for your consent rather than burying it in an update.
