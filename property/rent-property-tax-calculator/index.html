What does property tax cost per rental unit?

Property tax is one of a landlord's operating costs, but rent is not earmarked dollar-for-dollar to taxes. This tool makes a narrower, defensible comparison: the building's property-tax cost per unit versus the monthly rent you enter.

This is not a statement that your landlord sends this percentage of rent to the municipality. Owners also have financing, insurance, maintenance, utilities, management, vacancy and other costs, and rents are set by leases and market conditions.

Why this matters in New Jersey

Renters do not receive an individual property-tax bill, so the tax burden on the building is easy to overlook. Comparing the building's annual tax with its unit count provides useful scale without pretending the accounting relationship is more precise than it is.
