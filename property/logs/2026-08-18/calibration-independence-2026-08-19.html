Calibration independence hardening

Production audit found that Guided Calibration Review correctly concealed the model answer before a human label, but the full Calibration Control could reveal model score, confidence and predicted class inside an opened case before the reviewer saved an independent label.

Change

A new post-render independence guard now masks score, confidence and predicted class for unreviewed cases in Calibration Control. Governed source evidence, deterministic evidence and evidence coverage remain visible. Once a human label is persisted and the case reloads as reviewed, the comparison output may be revealed.

Why it matters

Future fresh holdouts must remain independent. A developer opening the full control page should not be able to use Watchdog's prediction as an answer key and accidentally contaminate calibration ground truth.
