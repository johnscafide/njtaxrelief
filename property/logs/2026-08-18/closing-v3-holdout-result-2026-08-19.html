Diagnosis

The v3 repair improved score diversity, but the holdout shows that the present feature contract still does not separate human Priority from Not Priority strongly enough. Aggregate class separation is strongest for permit-closure gap (+18.96 points in Priority), environmental encumbrance attention (+17.68), and closing-exception attention (+11.81). Flood/environment attention (-0.82) and title-constraint attention (-1.65) show essentially no positive class separation in this holdout.

A simple reweighting experiment that emphasized permit/environment evidence did not produce a threshold satisfying all three gates on this tuning evidence. That means the next repair should not be a threshold-only patch or blind weight tweak. The next immutable Closing Review version needs a better governed feature contract and then another fresh holdout. These 35 labels are tuning evidence only and cannot be reused as promotion proof.
