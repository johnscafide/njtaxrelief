## Area audited
Profile popover, notification popover, mobile viewport fit, readable secondary text, notification scrolling, critical touch targets, and safe-area behavior.

## Finding
`property/css/home/home-shell-fixes.css` still styled the Property Home profile/notification surfaces as compact desktop menus even at phone widths. Confirmed mobile certification risks included:
- profile sign-out at 38px high;
- Read all using only 6px padding with no 44px minimum target;
- profile secondary labels at 10px;
- notification address copy at 9px and timestamps at 8px;
- notification footer at 10px with no guaranteed touch height;
- a fixed 430px notification-list max-height without a dynamic-viewport cap on the popover itself.

On short phones or landscape this could make the overlay exceed the usable viewport while leaving important controls and metadata too small to tap/read comfortably.

## Change
Expanded the existing `property/css/home/home-mobile-audit.css` mobile-only layer at `@media (max-width: 820px)`:
- Profile/notification popovers now use 8px viewport insets and a `100dvh`-based max-height that accounts for top and bottom safe areas.
- Popovers use safe-area-aware bottom padding.
- Profile rows are at least 56px high; primary labels are 14px and secondary labels 12px.
- Sign out, Read all, and Change Intelligence footer actions now guarantee 44px minimum touch height and 13px action text.
- Notification rows are at least 64px high with 13px titles, 12px address text, and 11px timestamps.
- Notification lists now flex within the available sheet height, use contained vertical scrolling, and retain momentum scrolling on iOS.
- Empty-state notification copy was raised to readable mobile sizes.
