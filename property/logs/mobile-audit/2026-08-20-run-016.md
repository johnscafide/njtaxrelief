## Finding
A real certification risk remained in `property/css/home/05-marker-experience.css`. Several operational controls still inherited desktop-dense values on phones: `.sec2-kicker` at 8px inside the <=720px rule, `.hs-tool` at 9.5px, `.hs-copy small` at 10.5px, `.sc-data` at 10.5px, and history icons at only 34px square. These are not decorative microcopy. They label or activate property-intelligence drill-downs, so users should not need to squint or precisely target sub-44px controls.

## Typography/readability assessment
**FAIL before fix / PASS for audited surfaces after fix.**
- Section kickers promoted to 12px.
- Drill-down supporting copy promoted to 13px.
- Scorecard data links promoted to 12px and given 44px minimum height.
- History-row titles promoted to 14px and summaries to 12px.
- Inline tool actions promoted to 13px with 44px minimum height.
- History icons promoted from 34px to 44px.
- Chevron size promoted from 9px to 12px.

## Preserve-vs-redesign decision
**Preserve the drill-down architecture, redesign its mobile density.** The expandable signal rows are useful and already structurally appropriate for mobile, so replacing them with a separate card system would add complexity without improving comprehension. The correct mobile treatment is larger labels, more generous touch geometry, and full-width tool actions at very narrow widths.
