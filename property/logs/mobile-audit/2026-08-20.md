### Area audited
Touch-target sizing for dashboard card controls, map drag/handle control, Leaflet zoom controls, and mobile map legend readability/fit.

### Finding
The dashboard's visual grid already collapses for mobile, but several critical interactions remained sized for a mouse rather than a finger. In the base dashboard stylesheet, `.wd4-card-menu` is 26x26px, `.wd4-map-handle` is 25x25px, and Leaflet zoom controls are 36x36px. The map legend also uses 8.5-8.7px text with a fixed minimum width. Those controls and labels are acceptable desktop densities but fail the mobile certification standard for reliable touch and readable map interaction.

### Change
Updated the existing mobile-only `property/css/dashboard/dashboard-mobile-audit.css` at `<=768px`:
- Increased `.wd4-card-menu` to 44x44px and added `touch-action: manipulation`.
- Increased `.wd4-map-handle` to 44x44px and added `touch-action: manipulation`.
- Increased Leaflet zoom buttons from 36x36px to 44x44px with a larger 20px symbol size.
- Made the map legend viewport-safe with `max-width: calc(100% - 20px)` instead of relying on a fixed minimum width.
- Increased legend row height, dot size, and label/value text to 11px for phone readability.
- Added a narrow-phone <=420px legend inset so the overlay remains inside the card edges.
