## Finding
A substantive mobile certification risk existed in the current Pro hero. The existing `<=560px` rules reduced the floating intelligence signals to 8px text and 22px icon circles while preserving a 410px-tall absolute-positioned orbit. That presentation technically fit, but it did not meet the current mobile readability standard and remained visually dependent on a desktop composition. Several conversion controls inherited similarly undersized typography: fast-plan category labels at 8px, `/mo` text at 10px, pricing note at 11px, price jump at 10px, floating price CTA at 10px, and core/origin labels at 9–11px. The hero buttons also inherited 12px control text.

## Typography/readability assessment
**FAIL before fix / PASS for the audited hero and origin surfaces after fix.**
- Hero supporting copy remains 17px with 1.55 line-height.
- Primary CTA text is 15px with 48px minimum control height.
- Fast-pricing category labels are now 12px, plan names 16px, prices 23–25px, `/mo` 12px, and supporting pricing note 13px.
- Price jump and floating price CTA are now 14px with 44–48px minimum touch heights.
- Intelligence preview labels are now 12–14px rather than 8–10px.
- Origin-card eyebrow is 12px, supporting copy 14px, and context chips are 13px with 44px minimum height.
- Important hero information no longer requires pinch zoom.

## Preserve-vs-redesign decision
**Preserve the hero content and all five intelligence signals; redesign the mobile visual.** The absolute desktop orbit is a strong desktop effect but translates poorly to a narrow phone. On mobile it is now a native card/grid preview: the core property-intelligence card becomes a normal-flow full-width card, the five signals become readable 48px+ cards in a two-column grid, and the layout collapses to one column below 420px. Decorative orbit rings and the redundant scroll cue are removed on mobile only. No evidence or conversion content is removed.
