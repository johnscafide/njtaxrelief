Mobile supporting text should sit in the 12–15px range, primary explanatory copy around 14–16px, form controls at 16px, and final actions at 44px+.

Do not hide or collapse the final review into a reduced summary. Instead, apply a route-scoped mobile presentation with readable typography, larger touch controls, wrapped values, bounded proof/creative preview height, and accessible async state announcements. Desktop remains unchanged.
