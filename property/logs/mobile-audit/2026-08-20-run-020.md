# Watchdog Mobile Audit — Run 020

- **Timestamp:** 2026-08-20 16:38 ET
- **Page:** `/property/pro`
- **Area audited:** Interactive product-story/app mockup and professional role switcher
- **Program:** Linear NJW-246

## Finding

The Pro page was structurally responsive in these sections, but the phone presentation still preserved a miniature desktop simulation. The product-story mockup inherited 6–10px operational labels across app tabs, property metrics, Watchdog Score evidence chips, watchlist events, research cards and action nodes. The professional role switcher also inherited 8–10px labels in tabs, role kickers, pills and visual examples. This technically fit inside the viewport, but did not meet the mobile readability standard and would require users to visually decode desktop-scale UI on a phone.

## Typography/readability assessment

**Failed before this run.** Operational text was materially below the current Watchdog mobile certification baseline. Important labels were commonly 7–10px, supporting state text was 8–9px, and several interactive role controls used 10px copy. These are not decorative-only labels; they explain the product workflow and professional use cases.

After the change, mobile operational labels are generally 11–14px, supporting copy 12–13px, body copy 15–16px, and interactive role controls 14px with 44–48px minimum touch targets.

## Preserve vs. redesign decision

**Redesign for mobile, preserve content.** The story and role content are core Pro conversion/education surfaces, so hiding them would remove important product meaning. Instead of shrinking the desktop presentation, the mobile layer now:

- removes the simulated desktop sidebar from the phone property mockup;
- presents product tabs as a readable horizontal snap scroller;
- converts the Action workflow to a two-column mobile card grid;
- enlarges all meaningful app-simulation labels and supporting copy;
- converts the five professional role tabs into a 48px touch-target horizontal snap row;
- removes decorative transforms from mobile role visuals and increases their internal labels;
- keeps all five role panels and all product-story states available.

## Change

Updated `property/css/pro-buying-path.css` in the existing mobile audit branch with a new **Run 020** mobile-only layer at `max-width: 720px`, plus narrow-phone adjustments at `max-width: 420px`.
