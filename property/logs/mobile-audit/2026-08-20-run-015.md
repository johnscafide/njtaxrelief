## Area audited
Final Property Home hero and figure readability, revaluation/reassessment summary typography, tooltip viewport behavior, narrow-phone metric presentation, and preservation-vs-redesign of desktop metric density.

## Finding
The page-level mobile audit still exposed a meaningful typography certification risk in the most visible property summary surfaces. The desktop report stylesheet defines the hero property subtitle at 13.5px, valuation supporting text at 12.5px, figure labels and figure subtext at 11px, and Revaluation Radar score labels at 10px. The summary figure strip also remains a desktop flex row, which can technically wrap but becomes uneven and difficult to scan on narrow phones.

These are not decorative-only labels: they identify the property, explain valuation context, name primary metrics, and communicate risk status. The content therefore must remain available on mobile, but its density needed a mobile-specific presentation.

## Typography/readability assessment
**FAIL before fix / PASS for audited surfaces after fix.**
- Hero property supporting copy is now 15px with a 1.45 line-height.
- Valuation supporting text is now 13px.
- Primary figure labels and figure subtext are now 12px with explicit line-height and wrapping.
- Revaluation score labels and reassessment headings are now 12px.
- Revaluation meter labels remain 13px with wrapping enabled.
- Tooltip explanatory copy is now 14px and constrained to the available phone viewport.

## Preserve-vs-redesign decision
**Preserve evidence, redesign the metric strip for mobile.** The property summary and risk metrics are core Watchdog evidence and should not be hidden. Instead of retaining the desktop flex-row behavior, the mobile figure strip is now a deterministic two-column grid, becoming a single-column stack at <=480px. This keeps labels, values, and supporting context readable without pinch-zoom or awkward horizontal compression.
