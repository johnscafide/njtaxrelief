    How points work

    • Sold! and Town Shapes: 100 for a first-try win, 15 less for each extra guess.
    • Pin Drop: 100 for the exact property, less with distance, minus 5 for each extra clue.
    • Lineup: 100, 75, 50 or 25 depending on how many tries you need.
    • Blocks: 100 for a clean solve, 20 less for each mistake.
    • Fair or Unfair: 20 for each right call.
