How to play
  • This is a real New Jersey home that sold recently. Guess the sale price.
  • You get six guesses. Each one tells you if you're too high or too low, and by how much.
  • Land within 5% of the real price to win. Within 15% shows up as close.
  • Every wrong guess unlocks a hint from the public record.
  • One new home every day at midnight. Everyone gets the same one.

    What this game teaches

    Towns don't tax your home on what it would sell for. They tax its assessed value, and every town assesses at a different share of market value. Divide the assessed value by the town's ratio and you land close to the sale price.

    That same math is how you check whether your own assessment looks too high.
