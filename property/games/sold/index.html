    How to play

    A real New Jersey home sold recently. Guess the price in six tries.

    • Type a price on the keypad and hit Guess.
    • Each guess flips to show if you're too high or too low, and by how much.
    • Every miss opens a clue from the public record.

        What this teaches

        Towns don't tax a home on what it sells for. They tax its assessed value, and each town assesses at a different share of market value. Divide the assessed value by the town's ratio and you land close to the sale price. That's the same math you'd use to check your own assessment.
