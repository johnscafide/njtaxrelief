      Tax rates are the state's 2025 general tax rates. Sale prices come from verified arm's-length residential sales since 2024. Assessment ratios are the state's 2026 Chapter 123 average ratios. Populations are from the 2020 Census.

      How to play

      Five towns, one real number. Put them in order from highest to lowest. You get four tries.

      • Drag a town by its handle, or use the arrows to move it up or down.
      • Hit Check. Green means the right spot, and it locks in place. Gold means one spot off. Slate means further away.
      • Each day ranks a different number: tax rate, median sale price, price per square foot, population or assessment ratio.

      A new lineup drops every day at midnight. Everyone plays the same one.
