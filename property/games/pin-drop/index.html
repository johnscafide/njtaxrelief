How to play

A real New Jersey home sold recently. Find it on the map. One guess.

  • You get three clues free: the sale, the house and its yearly property tax.
  • Stuck? Open more clues: the county, the town's size, its outline, its name and the street. Each one costs 5 points.
  • Zoom in to street level and the property lines appear. Tap the house you think it is, then lock in your guess.

Scoring

The exact property scores 100. Anything else scores up to 95, less the farther away you are: about 86 at a mile, 58 at five miles and 35 at ten. Extra clues come off the top.

A new home drops every day at midnight. Everyone plays the same one.

Past puzzles

Sales are verified arm's-length sales from the state's SR-1A deed records. Property lines come from the New Jersey parcel map. Past puzzles don't change your streak.

    What this teaches

    Every piece of land in New Jersey has a block and lot on its town's tax map, and that parcel is what gets assessed and taxed. Two similar houses on the same street can carry different assessments, which is why it pays to check your own.
