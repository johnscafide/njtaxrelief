    Town groups come straight from the state's list of all 564 New Jersey municipalities. Tax and housing words come from the Watchdog glossary, where every term cites an official source. Every board has exactly one solution.

    How to play

    Find four groups of four tiles that share something.

    • Pick four tiles and hit Submit.
    • Three of four right? You'll hear "One away."
    • You can make four mistakes. Watch for tiles that look like they fit two groups. Only one way works for all sixteen.
