      Past puzzles

      Your progress and streak are saved on this device. Past puzzles don't change your streak. Outlines come from NJ Office of GIS municipal boundaries; tax rates from the Division of Taxation. New to the terms? Open the glossary.

      How to play

      The outline is one of New Jersey's 564 towns. Name it in six tries.

      • Start typing and pick a town from the list.
      • Each miss shows how many miles away the answer is, an arrow pointing toward it, and how close you are.
      • Every miss also flips over a clue: town type, county, population, tax rate (the general rate per $100 of assessed value), then the first letter.

          What this teaches

          New Jersey has 564 towns, and each one sets its own tax rate every year. Two towns that share a border can send very different bills for the same kind of house.
