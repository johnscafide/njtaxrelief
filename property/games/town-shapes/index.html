How to play
  • The outline is one of New Jersey's 564 towns. Name it.
  • You get six guesses. Each wrong guess shows how many miles off you are and which direction the answer is.
  • Every wrong guess also unlocks a hint: town type, county, population, tax rate, then the first letter.
  • One new town every day at midnight. Everyone gets the same one.
