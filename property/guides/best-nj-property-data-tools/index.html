How to choose

Start with geography, then evidence, then workflow.

If you work nationwide, a New Jersey-only product is the wrong primary database. If you work almost entirely in New Jersey, national breadth may be less useful than local tax, ratio, municipal and appeal depth. If you need the source document itself, use the responsible government publisher. If parcel geometry is the job, use a parcel/GIS specialist. The mistake is buying the longest feature list instead of the tool aligned to the decision you actually make.

Watchdog is designed to win narrowly

  • New Jersey tax and assessment research
  • Chapter 123 and appeal context
  • Municipal and governed source provenance
  • Professional workflows for agents, attorneys, lenders, appraisers and investors

Watchdog intentionally does not claim to win everywhere

  • No nationwide property-data claim
  • No owner-contact enrichment sold as a core product
  • No replacement for county boards or official source records
  • No claim that Watchdog replaces a consumer listing marketplace or enterprise GIS stack
