5. First 30 days

Month one is about distribution infrastructure, repetition and learning. It is not about chasing follower counts.

$0 target spend
Week 1 · Foundation

Make every click measurable.

  1. Standardize `utm_campaign=watchdog_launch_2026`.
  2. Record baseline lookups, Score views, signups and profession-page visits.
  3. Complete NJPropertyTaxRelief cross-promotion placements.
  4. Confirm LinkedIn Page, social bios and profile links point to Watchdog.
  5. Create the first 12 reusable launch assets.
  6. Queue 2 Insights articles from current Watchdog data.
Week 2 · Distribution

Publish everywhere that fits.

  1. 3 LinkedIn posts.
  2. 4 short-form videos repurposed across platforms.
  3. 2 Insights posts.
  4. 5 useful community answers/posts where rules allow.
  5. 5 professional screen-share demos.
  6. 1 consent-based email roundup if the audience is ready.
Week 3 · Proof

Turn usage into credibility.

  1. Publish one real workflow/case example with permission.
  2. Pitch one local data story to at least 5 relevant outlets/reporters.
  3. Run one brokerage education session or schedule the next available slot.
  4. Create shareable property-score examples.
  5. Turn the week's top questions into two new pieces of content.
Week 4 · Double down

Keep winners, cut noise.

  1. Rank channels by meaningful Watchdog actions, not impressions.
  2. Repeat the top 2 content themes.
  3. Stop formats producing attention with no product action.
  4. Only consider a $5–$10 boost if one organic asset clearly wins.
  5. Set month-two targets from real conversion data.

6. 90-day campaign sequence

Build compounding assets first, then add proof and partnerships. Do not jump to paid acquisition because organic growth feels slower.

Three phases

Days 1–30 · Establish

Owned-site promotion, UTM discipline, profiles, repeatable short-form content, Insights cadence, professional demos and baseline measurement.

Spend target $0

Days 31–60 · Prove

Publish case examples, deepen profession-specific content, pitch the first data story, start regular email, collect real quotes and identify the first repeatable acquisition path.

Optional spend ≤ $20

Days 61–90 · Compound

Scale the two best channels, repeat the best content pillars, expand office/partner distribution and test a referral mechanic only if real usage is strong enough.

Keep total < $100

13. UTM standard

Every external campaign link should use the same naming logic so Analytics can compare channels cleanly.

Attribution discipline
Required: `utm_source` = platform or partner, `utm_medium` = organic_social / referral / email / owned / earned / paid_social, `utm_campaign=watchdog_launch_2026`, and `utm_content` = the specific creative, article, banner, QR code or post. Do not invent a new campaign name for every post.

14. Spend gates and stop rules

Small budgets disappear quickly when a weak campaign is allowed to run because the dollar amount feels harmless.

Protect the budget
Do not spend if:
tracking is missing; the landing page is unclear; the creative has not worked organically; the audience is broad; the goal is follower count; the offer is not ready; or the same $10 could put Watchdog in front of a room of real professionals instead.
A $5–$10 test can earn approval if:
the asset materially beats its normal organic baseline; there is one specific audience; the CTA maps to a measurable Watchdog action; a lifetime or hard spend cap is set; and the result will change what we do next.

15. Campaign guardrails

Protect Watchdog's credibility while we grow.

Trust first

No outcome guarantees

A Watchdog Score does not guarantee an appeal result, tax reduction, sale outcome, lending decision or investment return.

No manufactured proof

No fake testimonials, incentivized positive reviews, bought followers, purchased backlinks or coordinated engagement.

No list buying

Use valid marketing consent. Keep transactional email and promotional email purposes separate.

No community spam

Follow local group rules. Be useful without demanding a click. Leave if self-promotion is not welcome.

No fake local presence

Do not create a Google or local-business listing that Watchdog is not eligible to have.

No unverified provider claims

Marketing Studio can support planning and creative work, but this campaign must not pretend a provider send or spend path is certified when it is not.
