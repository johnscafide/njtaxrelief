Assumptions used

    Method and official New Jersey rule

    The 2024 New Jersey Assessors' Handbook states that a completed new structure, addition, or alteration is valued as of the first day of the month following completion. Its example shows an April 17 completion taxed for eight months, May through December. The forecast therefore prorates only the estimated added assessment for the remaining full months of the completion year, then applies the projected full assessment for the following full year.
