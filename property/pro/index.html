Questions.

How does the free trial work?

Agent and Professional each start with 14 days free. You add a card, and nothing is charged during the trial. On day 14 your plan renews at the monthly or annual price you picked, unless you cancel in Account first. We email you a reminder 7 days before the first charge. One trial per account. Founding Lifetime and Teams have no trial.

What does annual mean?

Agent is $119/year and Professional is $479/year, billed once a year. That works out to about $9.92 and $39.92 a month.

Can I pay monthly?

Yes. Agent is $14.99 a month and Professional is $49.99 a month.

What is Founding Lifetime?

One payment, no renewal: $349 for Agent and $999 for Professional. Same property limits as the plan. It is limited and may be retired as Watchdog grows.

How is Watchdog Intelligence priced?

Agent normally adds it for $12/month. It is free for a limited time. Professional includes it.

How do postcards work?

Design and proof 6 x 8.5 postcards in Postcard Studio. One price per card covers printing and First Class postage: $1.79 on Agent and $1.59 on Professional. Cards that can't be mailed come back as mail credit.

Do I get anything for referring a pro?

Yes. When a pro you invite starts a yearly plan, you get one month of your plan free. It is credited once their plan has been active for 90 days.
