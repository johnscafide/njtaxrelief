Can I pay monthly?

Yes. Monthly pricing is $59 for Agent, $129 for Pro and $399 for Pro+. Use the billing toggle above to compare.

No. Watchdog AI is being built into the paid-plan roadmap. The page labels it as coming soon rather than treating planned intelligence as a live feature.

Not yet. We will not advertise a trial until the trial entitlement and expiration flow is actually ready. The homeowner property lookup remains free.

Pro+ is built for high-volume, data-heavy work. We would rather confirm the workflow fit than sell a $399/month plan blindly.
