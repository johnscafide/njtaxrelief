The decision point

The jump from $59.95 to $169.95 is mostly about prospecting depth, not just more reports.

The difference between Pro and Platinum is not simply 75 additional property reports. Platinum adds researched real-owner and contact capabilities that matter to investors, acquisition teams and prospectors. If you do not need owner-contact enrichment, paying for that tier may solve a problem you do not have.

PropertyShark versus Watchdog

Similar entry price, different job.

Watchdog currently lists Agent at $59/month, Pro at $129/month and Pro+ at $399/month. Watchdog is intentionally New Jersey-specific, organizing tax, assessment, Chapter 123, municipal, permit and governed property evidence into professional workflows. PropertyShark is broader and stronger for ownership research, title/lien history, comparables, foreclosure prospecting and owner-contact discovery.

Before you subscribe

Check the New Jersey coverage for the exact fields you care about.

PropertyShark says report depth can vary by area. Before choosing a tier, open a representative New Jersey property and confirm that the ownership, lien, mortgage, permit, foreclosure or comparable fields you need are available in that market. A published plan feature is not the same thing as identical field depth everywhere.
