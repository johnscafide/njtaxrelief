What changes by tier

Monitoring, exports and contact allowances scale with the plan.

PropertyRadar currently lists 10,000 monitored properties and exports for Solo, 25,000 for Team and 50,000 for Business. Included phone/email unlocks also rise by tier, and Business includes API access. Those features matter if prospecting and owner outreach are the main job.

Watchdog is narrower and NJ-specific

Watchdog currently lists Agent at $59/month, Pro at $129/month and Pro+ at $399/month, concentrating on New Jersey tax, assessment, municipal, appeal and professional property intelligence rather than national owner-contact prospecting.

PropertyRadar is broader for acquisition workflows

If nationwide data, monitored properties, owner phones/emails, online ads, direct mail and field prospecting are core to the business, PropertyRadar may justify its higher subscription price.
