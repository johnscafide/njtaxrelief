What the subscription buys

Price is only useful with the workflow attached.

PropStream's pricing page currently lists 25,000 monthly saves/exports for Essentials, 50,000 for Pro and 100,000 for Elite. It also lists a 7-day free trial. PropStream is designed around national property research, prospecting and lead workflows. Watchdog is narrower: New Jersey property intelligence, assessment, tax, municipal and appeal context.

Watchdog may cost less for an NJ-only user

Watchdog currently lists Agent at $59/month, Pro at $129/month and Pro+ at $399/month. That is not an apples-to-apples product comparison because Watchdog does not duplicate PropStream's national prospecting stack.

PropStream may be better value for a national prospector

If nationwide lead generation, large list volume and outreach tools are central to the job, paying for PropStream's broader infrastructure can make more sense than choosing a narrower NJ product.
