Regrid pricing in 2026

Regrid is unusual because “Regrid pricing” can mean a free parcel viewer, a $10 individual mapping subscription, an API plan, a one-time county/state download, or a five-figure bulk-data license. The right price depends on which product you are actually shopping for.

Bulk parcel data

The economics change completely when you license the dataset.

Regrid's official parcel-data page currently lists nationwide annual licenses at $25,000 for Basic, $35,000 for Standard and $65,000 for Premium. A single-state license is listed at $7,000 to $10,000 annually per end-user license. These products are for organizations that need parcel geometry and data delivered for their own systems, not for someone who simply wants to research properties in a browser.

API pricing

Regrid API is a third pricing model.

Regrid documents self-serve monthly API subscriptions and enterprise annual licenses. Self-serve access starts with a record allowance and per-record economics; enterprise licensing is for higher-volume requirements and additional datasets such as enhanced ownership, standardized zoning, matched buildings and matched addresses. If you need an API, use the API quote or plan, not the $10 Property App price and not the bulk-license price.

Regrid versus Watchdog

Regrid can be much cheaper for map-first parcel research; Watchdog charges for a different layer of work.

Watchdog currently lists Agent at $59/month, Pro at $129/month and Pro+ at $399/month. Regrid Pro at $10/month is the stronger value if you mainly need parcel boundaries, mapping, field notes, exports and nationwide land-data context. Watchdog is the stronger fit when the job is New Jersey tax, assessment, Chapter 123, municipal change, professional evidence and ongoing property intelligence rather than parcel geometry.
