Using Watchdog

Watchdog is a research and workflow product built from public records plus clearly labeled derived intelligence. It is not a substitute for the authoritative agency record or professional judgment.

Public records and derived intelligence

Government records can contain errors, omissions, reporting delays and later corrections. Watchdog-derived scores, estimates, rankings and signals are analytical outputs, not government findings, appraisals, legal opinions, lending decisions or guarantees. Material decisions should be verified against the linked authoritative source.

Subscriptions and billing

Paid plans provide the capabilities and usage levels shown at checkout. Agent, Pro and Pro+ subscriptions recur on the monthly or annual cadence selected at Stripe Checkout until canceled. Stripe is Watchdog's current payment processor for new paid subscriptions. Stripe's hosted Customer Portal is the self-service system for supported payment-method, invoice, plan and cancellation actions. Cancellation is scheduled for the end of the current billing period unless a different rule is explicitly shown during the billing flow. Team agreements may use separate commercial terms and are not currently open for self-service enrollment.

Payment information

Watchdog does not collect or store full payment-card numbers. Payment credentials are entered into Stripe-hosted payment surfaces and handled under Stripe's security and privacy controls. Watchdog receives the identifiers, subscription status, plan, billing cadence and transaction events needed to operate your account, reconcile access and provide support.

Refunds

Refunds and cancellation requests are governed by the Watchdog Refund Policy, the terms presented in the applicable Stripe flow and any non-waivable rights provided by law.

Permitted use

You may use the service for lawful property research and professional workflows within your plan. You may not bypass access controls, resell credentials, scrape around documented limits, reconstruct intentionally redacted personal information, interfere with service security, or represent Watchdog output as an official government record.

Prohibited eligibility decisions

Do not use Watchdog as a consumer report or for credit, employment, tenant-screening, insurance-eligibility or similar regulated eligibility decisions. Professional users remain responsible for the laws, licensing rules and standards governing their own work.

Availability and changes

Sources, formulas and product capabilities evolve. Material formula/source changes are versioned. A source outage may temporarily reduce or neutralize a derived signal rather than silently substitute unsupported data.
