What this calculator includes

The monthly estimate combines mortgage principal and interest with property tax, homeowners insurance, HOA dues, mortgage insurance and a user-controlled maintenance reserve. The upfront estimate combines the down payment with a user-entered buyer closing-cost assumption, then subtracts any credits or assistance you enter.

For a property-specific tax input, use the NJ Property Tax Estimator or start with a Watchdog property lookup. For definitions and New Jersey property-tax context, use the Watchdog glossary and town and county reports.

Why the closing-cost percentage stays editable: buyer charges vary by loan program, lender, title/settlement provider, prepaid items, escrow setup, inspections, credits and assistance. A single hard-coded percentage would create false precision.

Common questions

Is this only for first-time buyers?

No. It is especially useful for a first purchase because it separates cash-to-close planning from monthly ownership cost, but the same arithmetic works for any financed home purchase.

Does the calculator include PMI or other mortgage insurance automatically?

No. Mortgage-insurance rules depend on the loan program and transaction. Enter the monthly amount from your lender when it applies.

Why include maintenance if it is not a bill?

Maintenance is not a fixed monthly charge, but excluding it makes ownership look artificially cheaper. The reserve is a planning assumption you can change or set to zero.

Is the estimate a lender quote?

No. It is an educational budgeting tool. Use lender disclosures and settlement documents for actual financing terms, cash to close and closing charges.
