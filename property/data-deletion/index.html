Data deletion

Last updated: August 23, 2026

You can ask us to delete your Watchdog account and the personal information associated with it. This page explains how to make that request, including if you signed in with Facebook or another social sign-in provider.

How to request deletion

Submit a request through Watchdog Account Support while signed in to the account you want deleted. If you cannot sign in, use Contact Watchdog and provide the email address associated with the account. Use the subject Watchdog Data Deletion Request.

Include the email address used to sign in to Watchdog and, if helpful, the sign-in provider you used, such as Google or Facebook. Do not send passwords, access tokens, Social Security numbers, bank credentials or other unnecessary sensitive information.

We may need to verify that you control the account before completing the request.

If you signed in with Facebook

Using Facebook to sign in creates a Watchdog account through our authentication provider. Removing Watchdog from your Facebook account stops that Facebook connection, but it does not by itself guarantee deletion of information already stored in your Watchdog account.

To request deletion of Watchdog account data associated with Facebook Login, follow the deletion-request instructions above. We use the email address connected to the Watchdog account to locate and verify the account before deletion.

What we delete

Once a verified deletion request is approved, we delete or de-identify personal information associated with the Watchdog account that we are not required to retain. Depending on what you used, this can include account profile information, saved-property relationships, preferences, notes, onboarding responses and other account-specific records.

Public property records and anonymous property-level information are not account data and may remain in Watchdog because they are derived from public records and are not tied to your identity.

Information we may need to retain

Some records may need to be retained for a limited period where required by law, fraud-prevention, security, payment reconciliation, dispute handling, professional recordkeeping or other legitimate compliance obligations. When retention is required, we limit the retained information to what is necessary and do not use it for unrelated purposes.

Timing

We respond to verified privacy and deletion requests within the time required by applicable law. Our Privacy Policy describes our current privacy-request process and response periods in more detail.

Removing a social sign-in connection

You can also remove Watchdog from the settings of the social provider you used to sign in. Doing so may revoke that provider's authorization, but if you want the Watchdog account itself deleted, you should still submit a deletion request using the instructions on this page.
