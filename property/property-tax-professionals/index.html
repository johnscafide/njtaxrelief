What this guide is for

Keep tax year, ratio year and evidence source aligned.

Watchdog is especially useful to property-tax specialists when it connects the parcel record to statewide assessment, ratio, equalization, appeal and sale sources without hiding definitions. That can speed an assessor, consultant or appeal professional's first review.

The platform should make it obvious which fields come from source data and which are derived screening markers. Professional conclusions still depend on the correct year, taxing district, authoritative record and applicable procedure.

Boundary: Watchdog does not establish an official assessment, determine appeal merit, replace the assessor or county board, provide legal advice or substitute for professional valuation evidence.
01 · Workflow

High-value property-tax workflows.

Use the platform to normalize research and expose evidence gaps before making a professional conclusion.

01Review an assessment in context

Combine parcel assessment, local ratio/equalization, property characteristics and available sale context without skipping tax-year definitions.

02Prepare appeal or assessment research

Organize evidence categories and source lineage before the professional analysis of value, discrimination or administrative action.

03Compare properties consistently

Use common source vintages and definitions when screening peers, reviewing sales or comparing municipal patterns.

04Monitor material changes

Track assessment and public-record changes that can affect an ongoing client, municipal or appeal review.

03 · Hypothetical examples

How Watchdog assists tax research.

These examples are fictional and contain no real residential address data.

Appeal screening

A hypothetical property appears outside an expected assessment-to-market relationship. The practitioner checks tax year, ratio, property facts and supporting sales before deciding whether the matter warrants deeper work.

Assessor research

A public characteristic appears inconsistent across sources. The assessor uses Watchdog to identify the mismatch, then validates it in the official assessment workflow.

Peer review

A consultant builds a peer set but discovers mixed data vintages. The set is rebuilt with consistent tax-year definitions before any conclusion is drawn.

Evidence completeness

Watchdog shows an appeal-evidence gap. The professional uses it as a checklist for missing sources, not as a judgment that the appeal will succeed or fail.

04 · Before you act

Property-tax data checklist.

These questions help prevent year-mismatch and source-mismatch errors.

Are the tax year, ratio year and sale period aligned?
Is the parcel identity reliable across the sources being compared?
What source proves each material property characteristic?
Is this metric derived by Watchdog or reported directly by an authoritative source?
What current filing, assessment or county-board rule still needs official verification?
