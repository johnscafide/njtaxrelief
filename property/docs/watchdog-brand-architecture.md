# Watchdog brand architecture

**Status:** Canonical launch naming standard  
**Date:** August 22, 2026  
**Launch umbrella:** NJW-271  
**Score governance:** NJW-270

## Master brand

**Watchdog** is the company/platform/master brand.

Do not rename the entire platform to Watchdog Index, Watchdog Score, Watchdog Atlas, ROBUST, or a profession-specific product name.

Watchdog now spans property intelligence, professional workflows, Data Center, analytics, Marketing Studio, integrations, CRM context, reports, broadcasts, automation, and future APIs. The master brand must remain broad enough to hold all of those products.

## Product hierarchy

### Watchdog Score

The canonical 0–100 property/place decision-orientation result.

Preferred public construction:

> **The Watchdog Score, powered by the ROBUST Framework.**

Do not casually rename it “ROBUST Score.”

### ROBUST Framework

The governed six-dimension methodology beneath the Watchdog Score:

- **R — Recourse**
- **O — Overassessment Position**
- **B — Burden**
- **U — Uniformity**
- **S — Stability**
- **T — Trajectory**

ROBUST is methodology and explanatory language, not the master company name.

### Watchdog Index

The geographic and longitudinal measurement family for municipalities, counties, New Jersey statewide, and later other governed place/time scopes.

The Index should answer how a place is positioned and how that position changes over time. It must not be a naive average of parcel Watchdog Scores.

Municipal/County/State Index models must be independently versioned and distribution-aware. They should preserve dispersion, tails, coverage, and source lineage rather than collapsing everything into a simple mean.

Preferred examples:

- Watchdog Municipal Index
- Watchdog Camden County Index
- Watchdog New Jersey Index
- Watchdog Index, Q3 2026

Avoid “worst town,” “most unfair municipality,” or other verdict/ranking language as the default framing.

### Watchdog Atlas

The public exploration experience for maps, municipality/county/state Index readings, historical movement, Score explanations, and source-backed geographic comparison.

Atlas is an experience/distribution surface. It is not the master company name unless separately approved in a future brand decision.

## Naming doctrine

1. **Watchdog** remains the primary brand.
2. **Watchdog Score** is the recognizable result.
3. **ROBUST Framework** explains why the Score is what it is.
4. **Watchdog Index** measures governed geographic/time-series position and movement.
5. **Watchdog Atlas** helps the public explore those measurements.
6. Product names may have their own domains for redirects or campaigns without forcing a master-brand rename.
7. Internal abbreviations such as WD may be used in code, tickets, APIs, or CLI tooling but are not the preferred primary public identity.

## Domain doctrine

The final primary domain is a launch decision under NJW-271. The domain should serve **Watchdog**, not force the brand to fit a narrow product feature.

Product/defensive domains may redirect to canonical Watchdog destinations. Examples could include domains for Watchdog Score, Watchdog Index, or Watchdog Atlas, but acquisition and trademark review remain owner decisions.

Do not move production until the final domain is selected and the cutover matrix in `docs/watchdog-domain-cutover-runbook.md` is complete.

## Public language standard

Use language that describes measurement rather than accusation.

Preferred:

- “Assessment Uniformity: 62”
- “Watchdog Index increased 4 points since the prior release.”
- “The largest change came from Stability.”
- “Evidence coverage: 91%.”
- “The score identifies conditions that deserve attention.”

Avoid as default positioning:

- “Most unfair town”
- “Worst municipality”
- “Bad property”
- “Bad government”
- “Best place to live”
- claims that a score determines legal, lending, insurance, housing, political, or investment outcomes.

## Distribution hierarchy

The desired long-term public grammar is:

**Watchdog**  
→ **Watchdog Score**  
→ **ROBUST explanation**  
→ **Watchdog Index** for place/time movement  
→ **Watchdog Atlas** for exploration  
→ reports, APIs, integrations, CRM, embeds, partner listings, and editorial distribution.

This hierarchy should be preserved across launch pages, methodology, product copy, documentation, and future domain planning unless a deliberate brand-governance decision changes it.
