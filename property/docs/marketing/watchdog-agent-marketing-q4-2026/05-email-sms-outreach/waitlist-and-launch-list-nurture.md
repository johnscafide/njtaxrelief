## EM-W02: Oct 3 (Sat), Phase 0

Subject: What the MLS tax field does not show
Preview: A seller quotes the assessment as the price floor. Here is the number that answers them.

Body:

Hi [First name],

Your MLS shows two tax numbers for a listing: the assessment and last year's bill. Both are correct. Neither tells you what the property is worth.

Statewide, the median assessment-to-market ratio in New Jersey is 55.2%. That means in a typical town, a home assessed at $400,000 is being taxed as if half its value is on the books. In a town that just revalued, the ratio is near 100%. Same state, two different meanings for the same word.

So when a seller says "my assessment is $400,000, I'm not listing under that," the honest answer depends on the town's ratio, the town's Chapter 123 range, and what verified sales nearby say. Watchdog puts those three things on one screen with the source and date attached to each number.

That is the whole idea. The MLS tells you what is for sale. Watchdog tells you what sits underneath the listing.

The agent field guide walks through this with sample screens.
