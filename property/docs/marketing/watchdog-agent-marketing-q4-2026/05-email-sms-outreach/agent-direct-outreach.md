# Agent direct outreach: individual agents who engaged

Who: New Jersey agents who did something first. Commented on a post, followed an account, downloaded the field guide, attended a lunch-and-learn, stopped by the Triple Play booth, or created a free account and set their role to agent. Nobody who did nothing.

## Rules (read before sending anything)

- No cold texting agents. SMS only to numbers that ticked the consent box at signup or on an event form.
- Cold email only to business addresses, with a working unsubscribe and Watchdog's physical mailing address in every email. Personal Gmail addresses scraped from a profile do not count as business addresses; skip them.
- Honor any do-not-contact request immediately, across every channel, and log it the same day.
- DMs only on platforms where the agent already engaged with us or accepted a connection. One follow-up per platform, then stop.
- Never say or imply that Watchdog tells you who is selling. Property data is not seller intent.
- Never quote a price other than $59 per month, $590 per year, $1,499 lifetime, $1.79 per card, $29 Move.
- Placeholders in brackets. If a placeholder would look like a fact, delete the sentence.

Sender for email: John Scafide, personally. DMs from John's own accounts (@thetaxwatchdog on TikTok, linkedin.com/in/johnscafide, and the Watchdog Instagram and Facebook accounts once confirmed).

UTM pattern: `?utm_source=<channel>&utm_medium=outreach&utm_campaign=q4-agents-<phase>&utm_content=<asset-id>`.
