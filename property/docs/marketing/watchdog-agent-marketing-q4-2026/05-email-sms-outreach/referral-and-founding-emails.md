# Referral and Founding Agent emails

The referral mechanic is already live in the product and this is exactly how it works: when a professional you invite starts a yearly plan, you get one month of your own plan free, credited after their plan has been active 90 days. Invite link format: www.watchdogindex.com/pro?invite=CODE, from the Account page. Monthly sign-ups do not trigger the credit. It is a credit on your own bill, not a commission. Do not describe it any other way.

Sender: John Scafide, Watchdog. Footer: "Watchdog, [physical mailing address]. [Unsubscribe]." EM-R05 and EM-F01 are transactional.

UTM pattern: `?utm_source=email&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=q4-agents-<phase>&utm_content=<asset-id>`.

## Referral ask sequence for paying agents (EM-R01 to EM-R03)

Trigger: EM-R01 at 30 days after first successful charge (or the same day as EM-P02 if that sends first; do not send both). EM-R02 14 days later if no invite sent. EM-R03 30 days after EM-R02 if still no invite sent. Stop the sequence the moment an invite link is used.

Nothing to do unless you know someone. If you do, the next email from me is written so you can forward it.
