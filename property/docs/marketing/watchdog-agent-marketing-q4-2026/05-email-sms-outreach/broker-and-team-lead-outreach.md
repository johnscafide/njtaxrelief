Sender: John Scafide, personally. Reply-to: John. Send from a warmed domain, not the newsletter domain.

Cadence: EM-BR01 day 0, EM-BR02 day 3, EM-BR03 day 7, EM-BR04 day 12, EM-BR05 day 20. Stop the sequence the moment anyone replies. Phone and LinkedIn touches sit between emails per the daily block.

### PH-BR02: live call script

Opening (if the broker answers):
"Hi [First name], John Scafide with Watchdog. Do you have two minutes? I am calling New Jersey brokers about a 30-minute office session, and I want to know if it is worth your time before I ask for it."

If no time: "Understood. When is a bad time not a bad time? I will call then." Book it and hang up.

Gatekeeper handling:
"Hi, John Scafide calling for [First name]. It is about a 30-minute session for the office in [Month]. Is [First name] the right person for that, or does someone else handle training and office meetings?"
If they ask what it is about: "I do a short session for agents on how New Jersey property assessments work and how to explain them to clients. No product pitch in the session. I would like five minutes with [First name] to see if it fits."
If they take a message: leave PH-BR01 content plus email. Ask: "Is email or phone better for [First name]?"
Never argue with a gatekeeper. If they say no, say thanks and mark the office for email only.

Discovery questions (pick three, listen more than talk):
1. "How do your agents follow up with past clients today? Is there a system or is it whoever remembers?"
2. "When a seller quotes their assessment as the price floor, what do your agents say?"
3. "Who handles farm mail in the office? The agent, an assistant, a vendor?"
4. "When added-assessment bills hit in October, does anyone in the office call the past clients who got one?"
5. "Do you pay for any prospecting or data tools for the office now? What do agents actually use?"
6. "What do you want agents to walk out of an office meeting able to do that afternoon?"

Positioning (only after discovery, under 45 seconds):
"Watchdog is New Jersey property intelligence: the assessment history, the town ratio and Chapter 123 range, verified sales, permits, all in one property view with the source and date on every number. For agents, it turns that into a Monday desk: ten property reasons to reach out to people they already know. It does not sell names or guess who is selling. Property data is not seller intent, and we say that in the product."

Objection responses (short; full list in objection-handling.md):
- "We already have PropStream / a data vendor." "Keep it. That is national and wide. This is New Jersey deep: assessment, Chapter 123, revaluation. The session is useful either way, because it is about the tax bill, not the software."
- "My agents will not use another tool." "Most will not. The session is 30 minutes and they leave with a script for the assessment question. The five who follow up with past clients are the ones the pilot is for."
- "What does it cost?" "For the office, that is a Teams conversation, and Teams is controlled enrollment, so I am not going to quote you a number on this call. Individual agents pay $59 a month. The session and the pilot cost you a conference room."
- "Send me something." "I will send a one-paragraph confirmation with what to expect. The thing I need from you is a date."
- "Is this a list of people to call?" "No. No names, no phone numbers, no guesses about who is selling. Property reasons to be useful to people the agent already knows."
- "Not now." "Fair. January is when assessment notices mail and every past client calls. Can I put you down for the first two weeks of January?"

Close:
"Two shapes: a 30-minute lunch-and-learn at the office, lunch on me, or a 5-agent pilot for 30 days with no card. Which one is closer to useful for you?"
Then: "Good. Wednesday or Thursday, [Month]? What time does the office usually meet?"
Confirm: date, time, room, headcount for lunch, dietary notes, who introduces John.
"I will send a confirmation today with what to expect. Thank you, [First name]."

Do-not-call: if asked at any point, say "Done. You will not hear from me again," log it, and remove from every sequence the same day.

### Pilot check-in cadence (John or Coordinator, by email unless the team leader prefers a call)

- Day 0: kickoff call, 20 minutes, all five agents. Lesson 1 done live. Clients added before the call ends.
- Day 3: one-line email to the team leader: "[N] of 5 finished lesson 1, [N] added clients, [N] drew a farm." Nudge the ones who have not.
- Day 7: first Monday desk. Email the team leader the aggregate: cards surfaced by column, touches recorded. Ask: "Any card look wrong?"
- Day 14: 15-minute call with the team leader. Show the aggregate, ask which agent is using it most and why.
- Day 21: email: what changed in the product since day 0, one question about what the team needs that a single agent does not.
- Day 26: email to each agent (EM-B07 wording): keep going at $59 a month or $590 a year, add a card by day 30, nothing is charged automatically. Email to the team leader: "Here is what the five are deciding. If the team would rather do this as Teams, let's talk this week."
- Day 30: one-page summary to the team leader. Ask for the next step in one sentence. If it is a no, thank them and ask what would have made it a yes.
