Slide 1 (cover)
Headline: Chapter 123 in eight slides
Line: The rule that decides most New Jersey tax appeals. Example numbers throughout.

Slide 2
Headline: Start with the town's average ratio
Line: Assessment divided by market value, averaged across the town's usable sales. Published every year.

Slide 3
Headline: The corridor
Line: The law draws a band 15% above and 15% below the average ratio. That band is the common level range.
Line: Example: a 60% ratio gives a corridor of 51% to 69%.

Slide 4

Line: Its assessment divided by what the evidence says it is worth.

Line: $600,000 times 60% is $360,000.

Slide 6
Headline: Inside the corridor
Line: Same house, but the evidence says $700,000. Ratio 64%. Inside 51% to 69%. No change.

Slide 7
Headline: Why "higher than the neighbor's" wins nothing
Line: The board does not compare you to the neighbor. It compares your ratio to the corridor.
