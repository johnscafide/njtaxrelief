## 3. Daily 20-minute engagement routine (07:15 to 07:35 ET, every posting day)

Comments on other people's posts reach more brokers than John's own posts in the first month. Treat this block as non-negotiable.

### Minute plan

- 07:15 to 07:18: reply to every comment on yesterday's post and the last carousel. Answer the question, then ask one back.
- 07:18 to 07:30: leave 6 to 8 comments on other accounts, from the lists below. Two sentences minimum. Add a property fact or a specific question. Never "Great post."
- 07:30 to 07:33: accept connection requests from NJ agents, brokers, team leaders, association staff, and title or mortgage partners. Send one DM (section 5) to each new connection from the day before.
- 07:33 to 07:35: check the post for the day is scheduled (07:45 or 08:15), first comment text is ready to paste, image is attached.

### Who to comment on (build the list in Phase 0, keep it in a saved LinkedIn search plus a spreadsheet)

- NJ broker-owners and managing brokers: [names], target 30 accounts, priority tier.
- NJ team leaders with 5 or more agents: [names], target 30 accounts, priority tier.
- Association and board pages and staff: New Jersey REALTORS (about 53,000 members), the local boards John sells into ([board names]), Triple Play organizers. Target 10 accounts.
- Title, mortgage, attorney, and appraiser partners who post to NJ agents: [names], target 20 accounts. They are the referral layer and they ask good questions.
- NJ agents who comment on John's posts: add anyone who comments twice.

Rotate: Monday and Thursday brokers and team leaders, Tuesday associations and partners, Wednesday and Friday agents who engaged.

### Comment rules

- Add something. A property fact, a date, a correction stated kindly, or a real question. Two sentences minimum, five maximum.
- Never pitch in a comment. No links, no "check out Watchdog," no offer phrases. The profile does the selling.
- Never talk about a specific homeowner, property, or town in a way that could identify a person. Use "a house in a 55% ratio town" style examples.
- Never use the banned language from the brief in a comment, even when replying to someone who used it. If a broker uses prospect-list vocabulary, reply about "property reasons to call past clients" without correcting them in public.
- Do not argue with competitors or their customers. Compare the job, not the bill, and only when asked.
- If someone asks a legal or appraisal question, answer the public-record part and say "confirm with the county board" or "that is one for an attorney or appraiser."
- Disagree with brokers in private messages, not in their comment section.
- Reply to every comment on John's own posts within the day. Unanswered comments are the fastest way to lose reach.

## 5. DM follow-up after connection (send once, within 24 hours of acceptance, not salesy)

Thanks for connecting, [first name]. Quick one, no pitch: what is the property tax question your clients ask most? I am collecting them for a series of one-minute answers and would rather write about what people actually ask.

### Variant B: the useful thing

Thanks for connecting, [first name]. Since you are in [county], one date worth knowing: [nearest relevant date from the brief, for example "the ANCHOR and PAS-1 window closes Nov 2" or "added-assessment appeals are due Dec 1"]. If a client asks about it, happy to send the plain-English version.

### Variant C: the lookup offer (agents only, from Oct 12)

Thanks for connecting, [first name]. If you want to see what the record looks like on a house you sold a few years ago, send me the address and I will pull the assessment history, ratio, and permits and send back a screenshot. No account needed. Takes me a minute.

Rules for DMs: one message, then silence unless they reply. Never send a link in the first DM. Never mention pricing in a DM unless asked. For brokers and team leaders asking about Teams, say "controlled enrollment, talk to us" and offer a 20-minute call; do not quote a price.
