# YouTube ads: Watchdog Agent plan, Q4 2026

Follows `00-brief/product-and-brand-brief.md` and `paid-media-strategy.md`. YouTube runs in the Recommended ($740) and Aggressive ($1,720) tiers only. Its job is cheap, targeted views that feed the video-viewer retargeting pool and put John's face and the sample card in front of agents already searching for competitor names and NJ tax terms. It is not expected to drive trial starts directly at these budgets; count it in the retargeting math.

Channel: YouTube @watchdogindex [create if missing]. Link the channel to the Google Ads account so viewer audiences can be built.

## 1. Formats and lengths

- Three 15-second non-skippable spots (AD-Y-001 to AD-Y-003). Note: YouTube's bumper format is 6 seconds; the 15-second non-skippable format is the closest to a "bumper" that carries a property fact. Each script also marks the 6-second cut so the same footage can run as a true bumper.
- Three 30-second skippable in-stream spots (AD-Y-004 to AD-Y-006). The first 5 seconds must earn the view: a property fact or a plain question, John on camera, no logo animation.

Production: John on camera against a plain wall or at a desk, phone or mirrorless, captions burned in, product shown on a laptop screen or as a clean screen capture with sample data labeled "sample." Music optional and quiet. No stock footage of houses with people. Every script ends on the same end card: Watchdog wordmark, "Start your 14-day Agent trial," `www.watchdogindex.com/agents/trial`, and in small text "Card required. $59 per month after day 14 unless cancelled."

## 2. Scripts

### AD-Y-001, 15 seconds, non-skippable: the sample card
- 0 to 3 s (also the 6-second bumper open): Screen: sample property card fills the frame. On-screen text: "123 Example Street. Assessment up 11.4% since 2024. Source: MOD-IV, Aug 2026. Sample." John, voice-over: "This is what a New Jersey agent sees before the appointment."
- 3 to 6 s: Card scrolls to show tax history, Chapter 123 ratio, permits. VO: "Assessment, taxes, ratio, permits, verified sales." (6-second cut ends here with the wordmark.)
- 6 to 11 s: John on camera, brief. "Every number has its source and date. That is the whole point."
- 11 to 15 s: End card. VO: "Watchdog. Start your 14-day Agent trial at watchdogindex.com slash agents slash trial."
- On-screen text throughout: "Sample data" bottom left. End card small text: "Card required. $59 per month after day 14 unless cancelled."

### AD-Y-002, 15 seconds, non-skippable: the Monday desk

## 3. Targeting

- Location: New Jersey, "people in or regularly in" the location. Language: English. Devices: all; exclude TV screens (agents watching on a TV will not tap a trial link).
- Custom segments (custom intent), one segment, people who searched on Google for any of: propstream, propstream pricing, propstream alternative, propertyradar, propertyshark, attom data, regrid, nj property tax records, nj assessment lookup, chapter 123 ratio nj, nj equalization ratio, nj tax appeal deadline, added assessment nj, nj revaluation, real estate farming software, real estate postcards nj.
- Affinity or in-market layers: none as a narrow. Optionally observe "Real estate agents" in-market to compare CPV.
- Placements (managed placements campaign, separate from the custom-intent campaign, 30 percent of YouTube budget): NJ real estate channels. Research placeholders, to be filled by the Coordinator before Oct 12 by checking subscriber counts and recent upload dates:
  - [New Jersey REALTORS official channel, if it has recent uploads]
  - [Two NJ brokerage training or market update channels, e.g., a Weichert or Fox and Roach market center channel]
  - [Two NJ agent-run channels with weekly market updates, 5,000+ subscribers]
  - [One NJ property tax or appeal attorney channel with recent uploads]
  - [One NJ real estate coaching or team leader channel]
  - John's own @thetaxwatchdog uploads mirrored to YouTube, if the channel exists
- Exclusions: content types Kids, Live streaming; topics Gambling, Politics; placements with under 1,000 subscribers.
- Audience exclusions: converters (`trial_started` and `subscription_confirmed` lists from GA4), current customers.
- Frequency cap: 3 impressions per person per 7 days on the custom-intent campaign, 2 per 7 days on placements.
