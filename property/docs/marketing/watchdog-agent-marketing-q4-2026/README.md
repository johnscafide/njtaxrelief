# Watchdog for New Jersey agents: Q4 2026 marketing kit

Everything needed to market Watchdog to New Jersey real estate agents, team leaders, and brokers from Monday September 28 through Thursday December 31, 2026. Copy is ready to post, send, print, or hand to a designer. Dates are exact. Prices and product facts were checked against the live plan page and the product code on September 28, 2026.

## Start here (30 minutes)

1. `01-strategy/master-plan.md`: the whole plan, goals, positioning, who we sell to, offers, channels, funnel math, risks, and the owner decisions with due dates.
2. `01-strategy/trial-decision.md`: the answer to "should I run a 7-day trial with a card that auto-renews," with the recommendation, the reasoning, and the exact product changes.
3. `02-calendar/master-calendar.md`: every day from September 28 to December 31 with what to do, when, which asset, and who owns it. Import `master-calendar.ics` into Google Calendar or Outlook. Open `master-calendar.csv` in a sheet.
4. `01-strategy/launch-readiness-checklist.md`: what has to be true before the doors open on October 12.

## How the pieces connect

- Every piece of copy has an ID (SV-012, LI-034, EM-T11, AD-M-007, PC-002, and so on). The calendar references those IDs, so a calendar row tells you the date, the time, the channel, the asset ID, and the file to open.
- Two doors open on October 12: the 14-day Agent trial (card required, $59 per month after) and the Founding Agent invite (30 days by code, 100 seats). Every channel points to one of the two. If billing is not open on October 12, run the invite door only; the copy already works for both.
- The Friday scorecard (`01-strategy/kpi-dashboard-template.csv`) is where each engine gets a kill or scale decision every week.

## Rules that apply to everything

No em dashes. No emojis. No buzzwords. No "leads." Property data is not seller intent. Only the prices in the brief. Placeholders in square brackets are things to fill in, never facts. Full list: `08-objections-and-compliance/compliance-copy-rules.md`.
