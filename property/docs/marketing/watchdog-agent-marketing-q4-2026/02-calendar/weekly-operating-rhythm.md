## Phase overlays

- Phase 0 (Sep 28 to Oct 11): add the launch readiness checklist items on top of the rhythm. Content pushes the free lookup and the launch list only.
- Phase 1 (Oct 12 to Nov 1): add lunch-and-learns (Mon and Wed afternoons), the Oct 22 webinar, and the first invite batches.
- Phase 2 (Nov 2 to Dec 6): add the referral contest, the Nov 12 roundtable, the Nov 19 webinar, ambassador selection Nov 16, and Triple Play prep. Thanksgiving week (Nov 23 to 27): posts Mon to Wed only, no outreach Thu and Fri.
- Phase 3 (Dec 7 to Dec 31): Triple Play Dec 7 to 10 replaces the daily rhythm (see the Triple Play plan). Dec 11 to 18 follow-up. Dec 14 year-end annual push. Dec 24 to 27 nothing scheduled except pre-scheduled posts. Dec 28 to 31 light rhythm plus the last annual push.

## What gets cut first if time runs short

1. Instagram Stories.
2. Facebook page posts (keep group posts).
3. Video to three a week (keep Monday, Wednesday, Friday).
4. Never cut: the Monday email, the broker outreach block, lunch-and-learns, the trial sequence, the Friday scorecard.
