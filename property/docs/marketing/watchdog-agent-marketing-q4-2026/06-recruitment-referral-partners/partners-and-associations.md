## Co-marketing partners: title, mortgage, attorneys, inspectors, appraisers

These professionals see agents every week and are trusted by them. The ask is not "buy Watchdog" (that is the separate Pro pitch). The ask is "tell the agents you work with."

What they can do:

1. Put a 100-word item and the /real-estate-agents field-guide link in their agent-facing newsletter or email.
2. Hand the Watchdog sell sheet (from 07-traditional) to agents at closings (attorneys, title), at pre-approval (lenders), or at the inspection (inspectors).
3. Co-host a January "tax season prep" webinar for agents (template below). They get co-host billing and ten minutes on their own topic.
4. Send an agent who asks about property taxes to the free lookup.

What they get: co-host billing, their name and link in the webinar invite and follow-up, a mention in the weekly agent email when the webinar is announced, and a separate Pro conversation if they want one. No payment either way, and no referral fee: the live referral reward applies only to professionals who start a yearly plan through an invite link, and it is disclosed if used.
