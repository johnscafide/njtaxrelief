## The 90-second demo (EV-043)

"Which town do you work most? [Town.] Give me a street you have sold on. [Types the address.] Here is the property. Assessment by year, tax by year, the town's ratio, and what the assessment implies as a market value. This score, 0 to 100, is the Watchdog Score, powered by the ROBUST Framework, and every component opens the evidence with the source and date. That is your two minutes before a listing appointment. One more thing. [Opens the sample Opportunity Desk.] Every Monday this shows you which of your past clients have a property reason to hear from you: an assessment change, a permit, a verified sale on their street. Not who is selling. Reasons. Agent plan is $59 a month. Scan this and start the 14-day trial, or ask me about a Founding Agent seat if any are left."

If the person is licensed in New York or Pennsylvania only: "Honest answer, this is New Jersey only. If you work New Jersey property, it is for you. If not, I will not waste your time." Take a card only if they ask.

## Qualifying questions (EV-044)

Ask in this order; stop when the answer says stop.

1. Are you licensed in New Jersey? (No: see above.)
2. Which county do you work most?
3. Sphere, farm, or both?
4. Are you on a team, or do you run one? (Runs one: team pilot, see team-leader-offer.md.)
5. Who in your office would want to see this, and would your broker host 30 minutes over lunch? (Yes: lunch-and-learn, see brokerage-lunch-and-learn.md.)
6. What is the last tax question a client asked that you could not answer? (Write it down. It goes into 08-objections-and-compliance and into the January webinar.)

## Consent-based contact capture (EV-045)

Our own form on a tablet (paper backup): name, email, brokerage, county, role (agent, broker-owner, team leader, other), and one required checkbox: "Yes, email me the follow-up from Triple Play and Watchdog's weekly agent email. Unsubscribe anytime." Two optional checkboxes: "Book a lunch-and-learn at my office" and "I am interested in a team pilot."

Rules:

- Nobody is added without the checkbox ticked by them, on the screen, in front of us.
- A business card is scanned only when the person hands it over and says they want the follow-up, and the checkbox is still ticked on the tablet.
- If the expo provides a badge scanner, it is used only when the person asks to be contacted, and the consent checkbox is still ticked on our form. No scanning badges in passing, no scanning the badge of someone who only took a giveaway, no scraping the attendee list, no photographing badges.
- No texting anyone from Triple Play. The form does not collect mobile numbers.
- The Coordinator exports the consented list each evening and tags it "tripleplay-2026" with the day.
