# Ambassador program

Folder: 06-recruitment-referral-partners. Owner: John. Coordinator tracks posts, office Q and As, and the monthly call. Selection Monday November 16 (Phase 2). First monthly call Wednesday December 2. Runs into 2027.

Ten to fifteen agents from the Founding cohort who already talk about Watchdog in their office and online, made official, with a disclosure line, a code of conduct, and a few things worth having. No engineering.

## Selection (by November 16)

Pool: Founding Agents from batches 1 to 3 (redeemed October 12 to 26), because they have had at least three weeks with the product by selection day. Batches 4 and 5 can join in January.

Criteria, all required:

1. Activated (the four steps in founding-agent-program.md).
2. Weekly active in at least 3 of the last 4 weeks.
3. Recorded at least 5 outcomes in the Opportunity Desk.
4. Already posts about their work on at least one channel, or already speaks at their office sales meeting. We are not asking anyone to become a poster.
5. Comfortable saying the disclosure line out loud and in writing.
6. Not paid by, or an affiliate of, another property-data vendor. Ask directly.
7. Agrees to the code of conduct in writing (reply "agreed" to RF-044).

Mix: no more than 2 per brokerage office, at least 6 counties represented, a mix of solo agents and team members. Ten is the floor; fifteen is the ceiling.

Process: the Coordinator pulls the shortlist from the metrics sheet on Friday November 13, John picks, invitations (RF-043) go out Monday November 16, acceptances close Friday November 20, the ambassador list is announced in the group chat November 23.

## What an ambassador does

1. One post a month, in their own words, on the channel they already use. A screenshot of their own screen with client data hidden, or a sample screen labeled sample. The disclosure line every time. John does not write their posts; the Coordinator sends a monthly prompt (RF-046) with a date hook and a property fact they can use or ignore.
2. Host one 15-minute Q and A in their office, once a quarter, at the sales meeting. They get the office Q and A kit (RF-045): four slides from the lunch-and-learn deck (assessment vs market value, Chapter 123, the 2027 deadlines, added assessments) and the five stock answers. If the office wants the full 30 minutes, they hand it to John and it becomes a lunch-and-learn.
3. Answer questions in agent groups honestly, with the disclosure. "Honestly" includes "it does not do that" and "I have not tried that."
4. Join the monthly call.
5. Tell John what is broken, first, before anyone else hears it.

Ambassadors are not salespeople. They do not have quotas. They are never asked to post something they do not believe.

## RF-040: The disclosure line

Ambassadors use one of these, every time, in the post, the caption, the video, or the message, near the top and near any link:

- Full: "Disclosure: I am a Watchdog Ambassador. Watchdog gives me free postcards and other perks, and I get a month free when an agent signs up for a year through my link."
- Short (texts, DMs, replies in groups): "I am a Watchdog Ambassador and get perks from them."
- Video: said out loud in the first 15 seconds and repeated in the caption.

Not acceptable on its own: "#ad," "#partner," a disclosure only in the comments, or a disclosure hidden below "more."

## RF-041: Monthly call agenda (45 minutes; first call Wednesday December 2 at 17:00 ET, after the office day)

1. What shipped since the last call (John, 5 minutes).
2. What each ambassador heard this month: one thing an agent said, one thing a homeowner said (2 minutes each, 20 to 30 minutes total).
3. The December post: the date hook is the December 1 added-assessment deadline just passed and the "expense it in 2026" annual plan angle; the property fact is that 2027 assessment notices arrive around February 1 (5 minutes).
4. Triple Play: who is coming, the dinner on December 8, booth or floor shifts if anyone wants one (5 minutes).
5. Office Q and A schedule for January (3 minutes).
6. The question John always asks last: what would make you embarrassed to recommend this? (5 minutes)

Coordinator sends notes and the post prompt within 24 hours.

## RF-042: Code of conduct (ambassadors reply "agreed" before they are listed)

1. Property data is not seller intent. Never say or imply that Watchdog finds people who are about to sell, predicts listings, or identifies anyone as a seller from public records. Say "reasons to reach out" and "property-triggered conversations."
2. No bashing towns. Never call a municipality unfair, a rip-off, the worst, or anything like it. Use measurement language: "Uniformity: 62," "assessment pressure above the peer band."
3. No owner-contact scraping. Never use Watchdog, or anything else, to collect owner names, phone numbers, or emails. Never post a homeowner's name or situation. Addresses on the public record are fine; people are not.
4. Disclose every time. The disclosure line goes on every post, video, DM, and group reply about Watchdog.
5. No client data on screen. Hide it or use the sample.
6. Prices only as they are: $59 per month, $590 per year, $1.79 per card, $29 for Watchdog Move. Nothing else. No Teams number ever.
7. No promised outcomes. No "win your appeal," no "cut your taxes," no "double your listings." Assessments are tax-administration values, not list prices.
8. Respect group rules. If a group bans promotion, answer the question and stop. Send links only in private messages, with the disclosure.
9. Do not trash competitors. Compare the job, not the bill.
10. Anything you mail through Postcard Studio carries your name, your brokerage, and your broker's required disclosure.
11. If you are unsure, ask in the group chat before posting.
12. One warning, then removal. Removal ends the ambassador benefits but not Founding benefits.
