# Watchdog for New Jersey agents: Q4 2026 master marketing plan

Window: Monday September 28 through Thursday December 31, 2026. Audience: New Jersey real estate agents, team leaders, and brokers only.

## 1. The plan in one paragraph

Open two doors on October 12: a 14-day Agent trial that takes a card, and a Founding Agents cohort of 100 seats by invite. Fill both with a founder-led content engine (daily short video, daily LinkedIn, Facebook groups), tight New Jersey paid media, and an in-person motion that does not depend on ads: twelve brokerage lunch-and-learns, two open webinars, and Triple Play in Atlantic City on December 7 to 10. Turn the first 30 paying agents into referrers using the referral reward that already exists in the product, then close the year with an annual-plan push agents can expense in 2026. Goal: 100 paying Agent accounts by December 31.

## 3. Positioning

Headline idea: **Walk into the conversation knowing the property.**

What we say Watchdog is: New Jersey property intelligence for agents. The evidence companion beside the MLS. It brings the assessment, tax history, Chapter 123 context, verified sales, permits, and town context into one view with the source attached, then tells you which past clients and farm properties have a real property reason to hear from you this week.

What we say it is not: not an MLS, not an appraisal, not a title search, not a list of people who are "going to sell." That last one is the point, and we say it out loud. Agents are tired of tools that promise seller intent from public records. We promise a reason to be useful.

Frame against national tools: they go wide and shallow across 50 states. Watchdog goes New Jersey deep: assessment mechanics, revaluations, added assessments, equalization ratios, the appeal calendar, and 564 municipalities. Compare the job, not the bill.

Three proof points we can say today: 564 New Jersey municipalities, 131,244 SR1A verified sales on file, 55.2 percent statewide median assessment-to-market ratio.

## 4. Who we are selling to

**Segment A: the sphere-and-farm agent (primary).** Three or more years licensed, 8 to 25 closed sides a year, works listings, has a past-client list they feel guilty about not calling, and a farm they mail to inconsistently. Pain: "I have nothing new to say when I call." Buys on: a reason to reach out every Monday, farm postcards done for them at $1.79, and looking smart about taxes at a listing appointment. Price sensitivity: moderate; $59 is a closing gift.

**Segment B: the team leader (3 to 15 agents).** Pain: agents do not prospect consistently and the team has no shared rhythm. Buys on: a repeatable Monday desk for the whole team, a pilot that proves it in 30 days, and reporting on conversations, not "leads." Path: 5-agent pilot, then Agent seats or a Teams conversation (controlled enrollment; never quote a price).

**Segment C: the broker-owner or managing broker.** Pain: recruiting and retention, agents asking tax questions they cannot answer, office training that is not a vendor pitch. Buys on: free tax education for the office, a tool that makes their agents look competent, and an office code. Path: lunch-and-learn.

Not our buyer in Q4: rental-only agents, out-of-state agents, anyone whose real ask is skip tracing or owner phone numbers, and agents who want a national list machine. Say no early.

## 6. Channels and why each one is in the plan

1. **Founder-led short video (TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts).** John already has a homeowner audience at @thetaxwatchdog. The agent angle writes itself: "your clients are learning this from my videos; be the agent who can answer." Five videos a week, batch-filmed twice a week. Files: 03-social/short-form-video-scripts.md.
2. **LinkedIn (John's profile plus a Watchdog page).** Where brokers and team leaders read. One post every weekday, one carousel a week, a 20-minute daily engagement block. Files: 03-social/linkedin-*.md.
3. **Facebook groups and page, Instagram.** Where agents actually are. Teach, do not pitch. Files: 03-social/facebook-*.md, instagram-plan.md.
4. **Paid media, New Jersey only.** Meta for reach and retargeting, Google Search for competitor and NJ tax intent, LinkedIn only at the higher budget tiers, YouTube pre-roll for cheap frequency. Three budget tiers: $4,500, $9,000, $18,000 for the window. Files: 04-paid-ads/.
5. **Outbound to brokers and team leaders.** Ten new brokers and ten follow-ups every weekday morning; twenty warm agent DMs every afternoon. The ask is a lunch-and-learn or a pilot, never a price. Files: 05-email-sms-outreach/broker-and-team-lead-outreach.md.
6. **Lunch-and-learns.** The core recruiting motion. Twelve sessions, thirty minutes each, free tax education first, offer last. Files: 06-recruitment-referral-partners/brokerage-lunch-and-learn.md.
7. **Founding Agents and referrals.** One hundred seats, batches of twenty a week. Referral reward already in the product: one free month when an invited pro starts a yearly plan. Files: 06-recruitment-referral-partners/.
8. **Triple Play, December 7 to 10, Atlantic City.** The biggest room of NJ agents in the year. Exhibitor if a booth is available by October 2, otherwise the attendee path with a Watchdog dinner. Files: 06-recruitment-referral-partners/triple-play-plan.md.
9. **Traditional.** Postcards to brokerage offices (using our own Postcard Studio where possible), association print, a launch press release, podcast guesting, radio only if the rate makes sense, and a sell sheet in every office we visit. Files: 07-traditional/.
10. **Email.** Launch-list nurture, the trial sequence, a Monday "Watchdog Agent Weekly," and event emails. Files: 05-email-sms-outreach/.

## 7. Funnel model (Recommended tier, $9,000 paid media)

Planning assumptions, stated as ranges in 04-paid-ads/paid-media-strategy.md. This is the midpoint case.

| Stage | Paid | Organic and content | Outbound and events | Referral | Total |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Landing page visits | 4,000 | 3,000 | 600 | 300 | 7,900 |
| Trial starts (card door, 4 percent of paid and organic) | 160 | 120 | 0 | 0 | 280 |
| Founding invites (no-card door) | 0 | 20 | 60 | 20 | 100 |
| Paid from card trials (42 percent) | 67 | 50 | 0 | 0 | 117 |
| Paid from Founding invites (35 percent by Dec 31; the rest convert in January) | 0 | 7 | 21 | 7 | 35 |
| **Paying agents by Dec 31** | | | | | **~100 after churn and timing** |

Card trials started after December 17 do not convert inside the window. Founding invites issued after December 1 mostly convert in January. The plan front-loads both doors into October and November for that reason.

Cost check: $9,000 paid media producing about 67 paying agents is roughly $134 per paying agent from paid alone, against $59 per month revenue. That pays back in three months at zero churn and in about five months at 10 percent monthly churn. Acceptable for a launch quarter. Kill rules are in the paid strategy file.

## 12. Risks and what we do about them

| Risk | Signal | Response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Billing gate not open on Oct 12 | Friday gate check says controlled | Run Phase 1 on the Founding invite door only. Do not weaken the gate. Move the card trial to the first Monday after the gate passes. |
| Meta flags ads under the housing category | Ad rejected | Appeal with the landing page. Keep copy about software and the agent's work. Never add housing-availability language. |
| The 25-property capacity objection | Agents ask in DMs and groups | Answer honestly: 25 monitored properties on Agent, plus lists and territories. Position it as the 25 relationships that matter this quarter. Log the objection count weekly for the product team. |
| Low trial starts because of the card | Under 1.5 percent of landing views after 500 views | Fix the page first (proof, terms, sample desk). Offer the Founding invite to anyone who abandons checkout, via retargeting. |
| Content burnout | Missed posting days | Bank scripts exist. Drop to 3 videos a week before dropping LinkedIn. Never drop the Monday email. |
| No booth at Triple Play | Booked out by Oct 2 | Attendee path with the Watchdog dinner on Dec 8 and a daily "find John" schedule. |
| Auto-renew complaints | Refunds above 10 percent | Move the reminder to day 10, add an in-app banner from day 11, review the checkout disclosure with counsel. |
| Data freshness claims | An agent quotes an assessment that is stale | Every screen shows source and date. Train it in lesson 1. Never claim "real-time" in copy. |

## 13. What "foolproof" really means here

Nothing in marketing is foolproof. What makes this plan hard to break is that it has three independent engines. If paid media underperforms, the lunch-and-learns still fill the Founding cohort. If the billing gate stays closed, the invite door still works. If content stalls, the weekly email and outreach keep running. Each engine has a weekly kill-or-scale rule, so money and time move to what is working by the second week of every phase.
