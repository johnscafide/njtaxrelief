## EV-005. New Jersey 2027 property-tax calendar card (giveaway)

Two formats from one design: wallet size (3.5 x 2, two-sided) and desk size (4 x 6, one side). The desk card has room for the note lines. The wallet card drops the notes.

Front title: New Jersey property-tax calendar, tax year 2027.

Dates:

- Oct 1, 2026: valuation date. 2027 assessments reflect value as of this date.
- Nov 1, 2026: fourth-quarter 2026 payment due (falls on a Sunday; practically Mon Nov 2; grace periods vary by town).
- Nov 2, 2026: PAS-1 and ANCHOR filing deadline for the 2025 season.
- Dec 1, 2026: added or omitted assessment appeal deadline at the County Board of Taxation, or 30 days from the bulk mailing of added-assessment bills, whichever is later.
- About Feb 1, 2027: assessment notices mailed.
- Feb 1, 2027: first-quarter payment due.
- Apr 1, 2027: regular appeal deadline. May 1 in towns with a completed revaluation or reassessment. Jan 15 in Monmouth, Gloucester, and Burlington under the alternate calendar.
- May 1, 2027: second-quarter payment due.
- Aug 1, 2027: third-quarter payment due.
- Nov 1, 2027: fourth-quarter payment due.

Note lines (desk size only): Always confirm deadlines with your county board of taxation. Grace periods are set by each municipality. Quarterly due dates fall on the first of Feb, May, Aug, and Nov.
