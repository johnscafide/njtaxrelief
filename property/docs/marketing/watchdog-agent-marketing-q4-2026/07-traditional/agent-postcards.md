Folder: 07-traditional. Channel: postcard. Asset IDs PC-001 to PC-004.

The idea is simple. Watchdog sells a postcard product to agents. So Watchdog mails agents a postcard, printed the same way, and the card itself is the demo. An agent who gets a clean 6 x 8.5 card with a QR code and a useful property fact on it has already seen what Postcard Studio does.

## Addressing: offices, not homes

- Postcard Studio addresses every card to "Current Resident" because owner names are not public in New Jersey. That is right for homeowner mail. It is the wrong salutation for a brokerage office.
- Office mail should be addressed to "Attention: Agents" at the office street address, or to the broker of record by name. The broker of record for a licensed office is public record in the New Jersey Real Estate Commission licensee search.
- Postcard Studio builds its list from a saved audience (a farm, a town, or an uploaded list). Whether an uploaded office list can carry an "Attention: Agents" line or a broker name instead of "Current Resident" is not confirmed. ## Building a lawful office mailing list

What goes on the list: brokerage office street addresses. Nothing else.

Sources (all public):

1. New Jersey Real Estate Commission licensee search (New Jersey Department of Banking and Insurance). Search by license type for broker offices and branch offices. Record: office name, broker of record, street address, city, ZIP, county. [verify the current search interface and export options]
2. Public brokerage directories: franchise office locators, independent brokerage websites, and local board office directories where the board publishes them publicly.
3. Watchdog's own knowledge of which offices booked a lunch-and-learn, replied to a broker letter, or visited the booth (these get flagged, not removed; a second touch to a warm office is good).

What does not go on the list:

- Agents' home addresses. Never. Not from the licensee search, not from a property record, not from anywhere.
- Any list scraped from a social network, a personal website, or an MLS roster.
- Any purchased list of individual agents without a documented source and permission basis.

Fields: office_id, brokerage_name, broker_of_record, street, suite, city, state, zip, county, source, source_date, drop_1, drop_2, drop_3, drop_4, response_flag, notes.

Build steps (Sep 29 to Oct 2, Coordinator):

1. Pull office records county by county. Start with the counties around the first three lunch-and-learn brokerages and the counties with the largest boards.
2. Dedupe by street address. One card per office, even if several licenses point to it.
3. Standardize addresses (USPS format) so the mailable count stays high. Postcard Studio only charges for cards that can be mailed and returns the rest as mail credit; an outside vendor may charge for every piece.
4. Tag each office with the drop it belongs to. Rotate: an office should get all four cards over the quarter, not four cards in one week.
5. Save the file in the shared marketing drive as `office-mailing-list-q4-2026.csv` [location placeholder] and log the source and date on every row.

## Design rules (from Postcard Studio's own tips, applied to our cards)

- One clear message and one clear call to action per card.
- Keep text and logos at least 1/4 inch from the edges. Anything past the trim line is gone.
- Leave the address block on the back clear. The printer puts the address and postage there.
- Use one QR code and the short URL so the response can be measured.
- Sample screens on the card must say "Sample" and use 123 Example Street.
- Watchdog's own name, physical address, and the disclosures go on every card. (Agents who mail through Postcard Studio must add their name, brokerage, and any broker-required disclosure. Our cards are from Watchdog, so ours carry Watchdog's.)
