# Print ads: placements and copy

Folder: 07-traditional. Channel: print. Asset IDs PR-001 to PR-009.

Read the shared brief first (00-brief/product-and-brand-brief.md). Every rule there applies here: sentence-case headlines, no em dashes, no emojis, only the two offer phrases, only the prices in the brief, only the two proof numbers, and the legal footer on every ad. Competitors are not named in print. Nothing in this file is a quoted rate. Every placement is "[verify rates and deadlines]" until a rate card and a signed insertion order exist.

## Why print at all

Agents are local. They read the association magazine in the office because it is on the desk. Board newsletters get forwarded by the office manager. The Triple Play show guide is in every bag. A well-made page does one job for this plan: it makes the name familiar, so the postcard, the lunch-and-learn, the broker letter, and the booth land on a warm reader. Print does not close. The QR code and the short URL do the measuring. Buy small, buy repeatable, and buy where the agent density per dollar is highest.

Buying order if the budget is tight:

1. NJ REALTORS e-newsletter and county board newsletters (cheap, frequent, agent-only readership).
2. Triple Play show guide and mobile app (captive audience the week it matters).
3. NJ REALTORS member magazine (only if the Nov/Dec artwork deadline can be met).
4. Business journals and Real Estate NJ last (broker-owners and team leaders read them; rank-and-file agents less so; use them for the broker letter follow-up, not for trial volume).

## Production checklist

- Confirm trim, bleed, and safe area from each rate card before layout. [verify]
- Every QR code is generated from the exact tracked URL above and test-scanned on an iPhone and an Android phone from a printed proof, not a screen.
- Every ad carries the legal footer. No exceptions for size.
- Sample screens say "Sample" and use 123 Example Street.
- Read every headline out loud. If it sounds like a brochure, cut it.
- Final pass: search the layout text for the em dash character and the banned phrases in the brief, section 6.

## Measurement

- Each asset ID has its own QR and UTM. Report scans and landing-page visits by asset ID weekly to the 02-calendar owner.
- Ask each publication for its audited circulation or send count so the cost per agent reached can be compared across placements. [verify]
- Print is judged on reach and recognition, not on trial starts alone. If a placement produces zero scans after two insertions, drop it and move the money to postcards.
