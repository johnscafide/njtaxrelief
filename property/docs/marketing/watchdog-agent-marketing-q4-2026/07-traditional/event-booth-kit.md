## Qualifier questions (5)

Ask these while the address is loading. They tell you which part of the demo matters to this agent.

1. "Which towns do you work most?" (Pull one of those next.)
2. "How many past clients do you keep in touch with, roughly?" (Opportunity Desk and monitoring up to 25.)
3. "Do you mail your farm, or is that something you have meant to do?" (Postcard Studio, $1.79.)
4. "Are you the one who gets the tax questions in your office, or does that go to someone else?" (Lunch-and-learn invitation.)
5. "Do you want a seat in the Founding Agent cohort, or would you rather just try it for 14 days?" (Which offer phrase to use.)

## Do-not-do list

Taped inside the case lid. Read it every morning.

- No badge scanning without the person asking to be scanned. If the show provides scanners, ask "Do you want me to scan your badge so we can email you?" and take no for an answer.
- No seller-intent claims. Never say "find sellers," "who is about to list," or anything else that treats an owner as a prospect because of a public record. Say "a property reason to reach out."
- No town bashing. Never "worst," "most unfair," "rip-off." Use measurement language.
- No guaranteed outcomes. No "win your appeal," no "cut taxes," no "double your listings."
- No prices other than $59 per month, $590 per year, $1,499 lifetime, $1.79 per card, $29 Move.
- No competitor names, and no comment on competitors if an agent brings one up beyond "they go wide, we go New Jersey deep."
- No pulling an address on the demo station and then saving it, emailing it, or contacting the owner. Nothing leaves the booth.
- No pulling an agent's own home unless they ask for it out loud.
- No texting anyone from the sign-in sheet unless Box 2 is checked. No emailing unless Box 1 is checked.
- No offer phrase except "Start your 14-day Agent trial" or "Request a Founding Agent invite."
- No promises about features that are in beta (BoldTrail connection) or not built.
- No alcohol at the booth. The dinner is the dinner.
