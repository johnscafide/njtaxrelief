# Sponsorships, local presence, and the broker letter

Folder: 07-traditional. Channel: sponsorships and direct mail. Asset IDs EV-020 (sponsorship ask email), EV-021 (sponsorship tracker), DM-L01 (broker-owner letter and envelope).

Scope: agents only. Local boards, CE classrooms, brokerage sales meetings, and Triple Play add-ons. Youth sports, community festivals, chamber mixers, and homeowner events are out of scope for this plan. If a board offers a community event package, decline it politely and ask for the agent-facing option instead.

Every dollar figure in this file is a placeholder. Do not commit to a sponsorship without a written price and a written list of what Watchdog gets.

## Q4 sponsorship options

### 1. Local board holiday parties and installation dinners (November and December)

- What it is: county and regional REALTOR boards hold installation dinners and holiday parties in Nov and Dec. Sponsors get a logo, a table, sometimes a two-minute welcome.
- What we want: a table near the door, the calendar card at every place setting, and two minutes at the microphone if offered. Not a speech. "Bring me one address" and sit down.
- Budget range: [placeholder per event, verify with each board].
- Boards to ask first: the three boards covering the first three lunch-and-learn brokerages, then the largest boards by membership. [verify names and event dates]
- Event dates: [placeholder per board, verify]. Phase 2 and 3.

### 2. CE class sponsorship (coffee and a five-minute intro)

- What it is: boards and schools run continuing education classes all quarter. Some accept a sponsor who covers coffee and gets five minutes before the instructor starts.
- What we want: five minutes, one address pulled live, calendar cards on the table, the sell sheet at the back. No pitch beyond the offer phrase.
- Budget range: [placeholder per class, verify; usually the cost of coffee plus a fee].
- Which classes: anything on property tax, appraisal basics, or listing practice. Ask the education director which class fits. [placeholder]
- Dates: [placeholder]. Phase 1 and 2.

### 3. Brokerage sales meeting sponsorship (breakfast for a ten-minute slot)

- What it is: brokerages hold weekly or monthly sales meetings. Watchdog buys breakfast and gets ten minutes.
- What we want: ten minutes, live lookups on addresses the agents call out, calendar cards, sign-in sheet with the two consent boxes. This is the lunch-and-learn in a smaller box.
- Budget range: [placeholder per meeting, verify; breakfast for the headcount].
- Which offices: the offices that reply to the broker letter (DM-L01), then the offices that scanned a postcard QR.
- Dates: [placeholder]. Phase 1 and 2.

### 4. Triple Play add-ons (verify every one with exhibitor services)

- Coffee cart sponsorship near the expo floor, with "Bring one address to Booth [###]" on the cups or the cart sign. [verify availability and cost]
- Lanyards or badge holders with the Watchdog wordmark. [verify availability, cost, and artwork deadline; likely earlier than the show guide]
- Session sponsorship for a property tax or listing practice session, with a two-minute intro. [verify]
- Show app push notification or sponsored listing. [verify]
- Budget range: [placeholder per add-on, verify]. Phase 3, decided in Phase 2.

## Evaluation criteria

Score each option 1 to 5 on each line. Anything under 15 total is a pass.

1. Agents in the room: how many, and are they New Jersey agents? A hundred NJ agents beats a thousand mixed.
2. Speaking time: do we get a microphone or a demo table, or only a logo? A logo alone scores 1.
3. Hand-off: can we place the calendar card and sell sheet in every hand?
4. Consent capture: can we run the sign-in sheet with the two boxes? If not, score 1.
5. Cost per NJ agent reached: [placeholder] against the postcard benchmark of $1.79 per card.
6. Timing: does it land before Triple Play or before the year-end annual push?
