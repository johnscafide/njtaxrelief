### Eight talking points

1. October 1 is the date that sets every 2027 tax bill in New Jersey, and almost nobody outside a tax office knows it.
2. "The town is high" is the wrong answer. The right answer is the assessment, the ratio, and Chapter 123, and it is public record.
3. The statewide median assessment-to-market ratio is 55.2%. What that number does and does not tell you.
4. The 2027 appeal calendar: notices about Feb 1, April 1 deadline, May 1 in revalued towns, Jan 15 in Monmouth, Gloucester, and Burlington. Always confirm with the county board.
5. Added assessments: the December 1 deadline that catches every owner who renovated this year.
6. Why the MLS and the tax record are two different stories, and why an agent needs both before the showing.
7. Property data is not seller intent. A property reason to reach out is a reason to be useful, not a prediction.
8. The postcard that still works: one fact, one date, one QR, addressed to Current Resident, with the agent's name and brokerage on it.

### What John will not do on air

- Predict who is selling, or describe any owner as a prospect because of a public record.
- Call any town unfair, a rip-off, or the worst. He will use measurement language: "Uniformity: 62," "assessment pressure above the peer band."
- Give a tax-appeal conclusion or legal advice for a specific property.
- Promise outcomes: no "win your appeal," no "cut your taxes."
- Trash competitors by name. He will compare the job, not the bill.
- Share any client's data, name, or address. Sample addresses are 123 Example Street.
