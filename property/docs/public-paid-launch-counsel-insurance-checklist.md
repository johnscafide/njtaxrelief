## Insurance / risk packet

### Quote / underwriting description

Suggested factual description for the broker/underwriter:

> Watchdog Property Intelligence LLC operates subscription-based property intelligence SaaS. The platform compiles public and third-party property information; normalizes and analyzes records; provides scores, signals, estimates, reports, monitoring, maps, and research/workflow tools; and serves homeowners and professional users. It does not provide certified appraisals, legal advice, lending/underwriting decisions, title opinions, insurance decisions, consumer reports, or government records. The platform uses hosted cloud infrastructure and third-party authentication/payment providers and may offer API/integration capability on higher tiers.

5. What retroactive date, deductible/retention, per-claim limit, aggregate limit, waiting period, and sublimits apply?
6. How are contractual liability, IP/media liability, regulatory/privacy proceedings, ransomware/cybercrime, and business interruption treated?
