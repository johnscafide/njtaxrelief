How to score Priority, Not priority, and Uncertain

These fake examples teach the decision rule only. Do not copy their numbers into real cases. Ask one question: does the evidence shown independently justify putting this property into the model's review queue?

EXAMPLE 1 · FAKE PROPERTY123 Sample Oak Lane, Example Township, NJ 08000
ANSWER: PRIORITY
FACTS
Prior assessment$285,0002025 record
Current assessment$352,0002026 record
Recent permitRoof + structural workOpened 5 months ago
Permit dispositionFinal status missingNo governed final inspection result
MY HUMAN LABEL
PriorityThe evidence itself gives a concrete reason for closer review.

Why: a material recorded change, recent structural work, and unresolved permit disposition together justify human review. This does not mean the assessment is wrong, the owner has motivation, the property has a certain value, or a legal problem exists.

EXAMPLE 2 · FAKE PROPERTY45 Routine Maple Court, Sample Borough, NJ 08001
ANSWER: NOT PRIORITY
FACTS
Prior assessment$310,0002025 record
Current assessment$314,5002026 record
PermitWater heater replacementClosed and finaled
Other change evidenceNone shownNo unresolved governed signal
MY HUMAN LABEL
Not priorityThe evidence does not provide a meaningful review reason.

Why: the assessment movement is small, the permit is routine and completed, and nothing shown is unresolved or materially unusual. There may always be more information in the world, but based on the evidence actually presented, I would not put this property into the review queue.

EXAMPLE 3 · FAKE PROPERTY78 Missing Pine Drive, Demo Township, NJ 08002
ANSWER: UNCERTAIN
FACTS
Prior assessmentNot availableHistorical value missing
Current assessment$338,000Current record exists
Permit signalPossible additionSource record conflicts with another observation
Final dispositionUnknownMissing authoritative resolution
MY HUMAN LABEL
UncertainI cannot responsibly decide from the evidence shown.

Why: there might be something worth reviewing, but key baseline and permit facts are missing or conflicting. Choosing Priority would require me to assume the unresolved signal is meaningful; choosing Not priority would require me to assume it is harmless. Neither assumption is justified, so Uncertain is the correct label.

The shortcut to remember:Priority = concrete evidence-backed reason for closer review. Not priority = evidence is routine, adequately explained, or not meaningful enough for review. Uncertain = evidence is too incomplete, conflicting, or ambiguous to responsibly decide. Never use Watchdog's hidden score as the reason for your label.
Your label is the calibration evidence.Use Priority only when the available evidence independently supports review. Use Not priority when it does not. Use Uncertain instead of guessing when evidence is insufficient or ambiguous.
