New Jersey's common-level range is generally plus or minus 15% of the district's average ratio. Revaluation/reassessment years and case-specific facts can change the applicable analysis. Verify the current ratio, range and filing deadline with official sources before acting.

How the screening math works

New Jersey explains that a taxpayer must prove an assessment does not fairly represent the true-market-value or common-level-range standard. This calculator uses the common-level ratio you enter to create a ratio-adjusted assessment scenario, then applies the General Tax Rate to the difference. It does not select comparables, determine admissible evidence, account for every Chapter 123 exception, or promise that a county board or court will adopt the result.
