Watchdog vs PropertyRadar

Prospecting and outreach vs New Jersey-specific property intelligence.

PropertyRadar is a nationwide prospecting platform combining property data, owner/contact information, lists, alerts, comps and marketing automation. Watchdog focuses on understanding New Jersey properties, assessments, taxes, municipal changes and professional risk/opportunity signals.

Choose PropertyRadar if…

You need nationwide list building, owner phones and emails, direct mail, email workflows, property alerts, dialing tools or prospecting automation for a sales organization.

Choose Watchdog if…

You need a New Jersey research workspace centered on tax assessment, appeal evidence, permits, municipal and environmental context and profession-specific intelligence.

PropertyRadar's strongest advantages

  • Nationwide property and owner prospecting.
  • Hundreds of search criteria and unlimited list management.
  • Phone/email contact data and outbound marketing tools.
  • Direct mail, email automation, dialing add-ons and integrations.
  • Mobile apps, comps, investment analysis and property alerts.

Watchdog's strongest advantages

  • New Jersey-specific assessment, tax and ratio context.
  • Appeal Evidence Strength and Chapter 123-oriented workflows.
  • Permit, municipal, environmental and source-change intelligence.
  • Profession-specific workbenches rather than one generalized prospecting workflow.
  • Derived Watchdog markers intended to surface NJ risk and opportunity.
Pricing context

PropertyRadar is priced as a full prospecting platform.

Verified August 2026. PropertyRadar currently lists Solo at $119/month ($99/month effective when prepaid annually), Team at $249/month ($199 effective annually), and Business at $599/month ($549 effective annually). Team includes three users, 25,000 monthly exports and 25,000 monitored properties; Business includes ten users, 50,000 exports and monitored properties plus API access. Watchdog currently lists Agent at $29/month, Pro at $49/month and Pro+ at $349/month, with discounted annual billing for those individual plans.

Switching from PropertyRadar

If your work is NJ-only, compare what you actually use.

You keep property research, saved-work workflows, monitoring and professional analysis. You gain New Jersey-specific assessment, Chapter 123 and municipal interpretation. You give up PropertyRadar's national prospecting breadth, owner contact data and bundled outreach automation. Watchdog is the stronger fit only when those tradeoffs match your actual job.

Bottom line

Use PropertyRadar to prospect and market nationally. Use Watchdog to investigate and monitor New Jersey property intelligence.
