Watchdog vs ATTOM

State-specific workflow vs nationwide property-data infrastructure.

ATTOM Property Navigator is a national property-research product backed by ATTOM's broad property-data warehouse. Watchdog takes the opposite approach: narrower geography, more New Jersey-specific interpretation and profession-oriented workflows.

Choose ATTOM if…

You need nationwide residential and commercial property coverage, standardized reports, search, foreclosure and mortgage data, AVMs, large list exports or enterprise data integrations.

Choose Watchdog if…

You primarily work in New Jersey and want assessment, tax, appeal, municipal, permit and environmental intelligence organized around the decisions local professionals actually make.

ATTOM's strongest advantages

  • Nationwide access across more than 150 million residential and commercial properties.
  • Property and comparable reports, sales, foreclosure, pre-foreclosure and mortgage search.
  • AVM, rental-value and flood-data capabilities.
  • Clear enterprise tier for white-label, CRM/SSO and development support.
  • Established national property-data infrastructure for organizations that operate across markets.

Watchdog's strongest advantages

  • New Jersey-first tax, assessment and Chapter 123 context.
  • Profession-specific workbenches rather than a generalized national report interface.
  • Municipal, permit, environmental and public-source change monitoring.
  • Derived signals designed to flag local risk and opportunity.
  • Lower-cost entry plans for individual NJ agents and professionals.
Pricing context

ATTOM's Professional plan is annual-only; Watchdog offers monthly and annual individual plans.

ATTOM currently lists Property Navigator Professional at $499/year, including one seat, up to 200 property/comparable reports and 2,000 list exports per month. Enterprise pricing is custom. Watchdog currently lists Agent at $29/month, Pro at $49/month and Pro+ at $349/month, with discounted annual billing for those three tiers.

Bottom line

ATTOM is the broader national data platform. Watchdog is the more specialized NJ workflow.
