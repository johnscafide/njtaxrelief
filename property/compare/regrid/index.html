Watchdog vs Regrid

Parcel infrastructure vs property intelligence.

Regrid is fundamentally a parcel-data and location-context platform with nationwide parcel coverage, APIs, bulk files and a property app. Watchdog is an application layer built around interpreting New Jersey property records and professional workflows.

Choose Regrid if…

You need parcel boundaries, standardized parcel schemas, GIS-ready files, zoning/building/location attributes, a parcel API or nationwide land data to power your own software and spatial analysis.

Choose Watchdog if…

You need a finished New Jersey research workflow that interprets tax, assessment, appeal, municipal, permit and environmental records rather than only supplying parcel infrastructure.

Regrid's strongest advantages

  • Comprehensive nationwide parcel data and geometry.
  • Production-ready REST API for parcel, zoning, building and ownership/location data.
  • Bulk county/state data and spatial formats for GIS and analytics teams.
  • Property App for fieldwork, parcel lookup and saved places.
  • Strong fit for software builders who need standardized parcel infrastructure.

Watchdog's strongest advantages

  • Finished NJ-specific professional application rather than a raw parcel dataset.
  • Assessment, tax and appeal intelligence.
  • Municipal, permit and environmental diligence.
  • Derived Watchdog signals and change intelligence.
  • Professional workflows designed around real estate decisions rather than GIS data delivery.
Pricing context

Regrid pricing depends on the delivery method.

Regrid publishes separate pricing for its Property App, self-serve API, Data Store and bulk licenses. Its public parcel export pages currently show $0.10 per parcel record for spreadsheet exports and $0.15 per parcel record for listed spatial formats in the examples reviewed. Watchdog's current individual paid catalog is Agent $29/month, Pro $49/month and Pro+ $349/month, with discounted annual billing on those plans.

Bottom line

Use Regrid to build with parcel data. Use Watchdog to work with New Jersey property intelligence.
