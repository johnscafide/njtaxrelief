Watchdog vs PropStream

NJ property intelligence vs nationwide lead generation.

PropStream is a broad national real-estate prospecting and marketing platform. Watchdog is narrower by design: it focuses on New Jersey property research, tax and assessment context, public-source changes and profession-specific intelligence.

Choose PropStream if…

Your priority is building nationwide motivated-seller lists, skip tracing owners, dialing, direct mail, email outreach, investor lead automation or running a national acquisitions workflow.

Choose Watchdog if…

Your priority is understanding a New Jersey parcel deeply: assessment, taxes, Chapter 123/appeal context, municipal changes, permits, environmental diligence and derived opportunity/risk signals.

Where each wins

They can also complement each other.

PropStream's strongest advantages

  • Nationwide coverage across more than 160 million properties.
  • Large-scale lead filtering and prospecting.
  • Skip tracing, calling, email and direct-mail workflows.
  • Investor calculators, comps and acquisition-oriented analysis.
  • Team plans and high export volumes for active prospecting organizations.

Watchdog's strongest advantages

  • Purpose-built New Jersey assessment and tax context.
  • Chapter 123 and appeal-oriented research.
  • Municipal, permit, environmental and public-source change intelligence.
  • Profession-specific workflows for agents, attorneys, lenders, appraisers and investors.
  • Derived Watchdog signals intended to explain risk, opportunity and change rather than only expose raw fields.
Pricing context

Watchdog starts lower; PropStream includes more prospecting infrastructure.

PropStream currently lists Essentials at $99/month, Pro at $199/month and Elite at $699/month, with lower effective monthly pricing on annual plans. Watchdog's available paid catalog is Agent $29/month, Pro $49/month and Pro+ $349/month. Teams is currently gated until the organizational product exists.

Bottom line

If the work is prospecting, start with PropStream. If the work is understanding New Jersey property risk and opportunity, start with Watchdog.
