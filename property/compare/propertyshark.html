Watchdog vs PropertyShark

Deep property reports vs New Jersey-specific intelligence.

PropertyShark is an established U.S. property-research platform with ownership, title, tax, permit, zoning, comps and contact information. Watchdog is built more narrowly around New Jersey property intelligence and professional workflows.

Choose PropertyShark if…

You want broad property reports, ownership and contact data, title documents, liens, zoning, mailing lists and foreclosure research across supported U.S. markets.

Choose Watchdog if…

You work primarily in New Jersey and need local tax-assessment context, Chapter 123 screening, municipal intelligence, derived signals and profession-specific research in one workspace.

PropertyShark's strongest advantages

  • Extensive ownership and contact information.
  • Official documents, deeds, mortgages, liens and title research.
  • Detailed property reports, maps, zoning and permit information.
  • Comparables, foreclosure research and mailing/data-list tools.
  • Long-established national research product with regional depth.

Watchdog's strongest advantages

  • NJ-first property-tax, assessment and municipal context.
  • Chapter 123 and appeal-evidence workflows.
  • Watchdog-derived risk and opportunity signals.
  • Professional workbenches for different NJ real-estate roles.
  • Change intelligence intended to explain what changed and why it may matter.
Pricing context

PropertyShark pricing varies by region and research depth.

For the Nassau County pricing page checked on August 10, 2026, PropertyShark listed Pro at $59.95/month, Elite at $79.95/month and Platinum at $169.95/month, with annual savings available. Report quotas were 175, 200 and 250 per month respectively, with up to 1,000 ownership reports and 2,000 list-export records included under the published benefit structure. Because PropertyShark varies by region, buyers should verify the pricing page for the market they need.

Bottom line

PropertyShark is stronger for ownership and document research. Watchdog is purpose-built for NJ tax and professional intelligence.
