Rules used by this screen

Senior Freeze

Age 65+ or qualifying disability; ownership/residency since Dec. 31, 2022; 2024 income ≤ $168,268 and 2025 income ≤ $172,475. The reimbursement basis is current-year property tax minus the established base-year property tax, subject to full program eligibility.

NJ Division of Taxation eligibility

Stay NJ · 2025 application / FY2027

Age 65+; owned and lived in the home for all of 2025; income ≤ $200,000. Published annualized maximums are $6,500 up to $100,000 income, $5,000 from $100,000.01–$150,000, and $4,000 from $150,000.01–$200,000.

NJ Division of Taxation Stay NJ

PAS-1 filing deadline: November 2, 2026. The State determines final eligibility and benefit amounts across Senior Freeze, ANCHOR, and Stay NJ. Program availability and amounts remain subject to State budget appropriations. This workflow does not provide tax or legal advice and does not predict approval.
