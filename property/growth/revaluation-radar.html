Evidence-first, not predictive
This is a research-priority screen, not a revaluation forecast or calendar. “Approved 2026” is shown only when the municipality appears in the Division's current approved revaluation/reassessment file. A positive ratio gap means recent verified residential sales imply a lower assessment-to-market relationship than the certified ratio; it does not mean a revaluation has been ordered.

What this screen actually uses

Certified 2026 Chapter 123 / Director ratios

NJ Division of Taxation certification dated October 1, 2025, as amended January 30, 2026. Watchdog's currently governed file contains all 168 municipalities in Atlantic, Burlington, Camden, Cape May, Cumberland, Gloucester and Salem counties.

2026 approved revaluations and reassessments

NJ Division of Taxation, Property Administration. The parsed governed file records 140 districts recognized for 2026 implementation; this page uses the list as known status, never as a prediction.

Open state approved list ↗
Verified-sale municipal ratios

Derived from New Jersey SR-1A records using state-verified usable arm's-length Class 2 residential sales. The current governed build uses 2025 plus 2026 year-to-date evidence and omits thin municipalities rather than guessing.

Open SR-1A source page ↗
Assessment uniformity

Derived from NJ Division of Taxation Measures of Property Assessment Uniformity. The coefficient of deviation is shown as context about consistency across a municipality's assessment roll.

Coverage is deliberately fail-closed. A municipality appears only when the governed certified-ratio file can be joined to a district with the required municipal evidence. Missing data is omitted rather than filled with a statewide or neighboring-town assumption. No owner identity, contact data, protected-class inference, legal outcome prediction, fee recommendation, or filing deadline is produced here.
