Revaluation Early Warning

Rank municipalities for the next two assessment cycles using certified ratio pressure, verified-sale drift, assessment dispersion, ratio-history direction and municipal budget pressure. Known 2026 approved revaluations/reassessments are separated from the forecast.

Watch priority, not a prediction
This is a 12–24 month research-priority model, not a probability or order calendar. It never says a municipality will revalue. The score is a transparent heuristic for where to investigate first. Officially approved 2026 districts are labeled as known status and are not ranked as forecasts.

Model v1 · transparent evidence weights
  • 30% certified Director/Chapter 123 ratio level
  • 20% current verified-sale drift versus the certified ratio
  • 20% assessment dispersion (Coefficient of Deviation)
  • 15% multi-year certified-ratio direction
  • 15% governed Municipal Budget Pressure

A recent large upward ratio reset is used only as a recency guard and caps the watch score. It is explicitly a ratio-history proxy, not an assertion of an official last-revaluation date. Missing inputs reduce evidence coverage; fewer than four supported components produces no forecast score.

PROVENANCE

Evidence boundary

Certified ratios

NJ Division of Taxation 2026 Chapter 123 / Director ratios plus Watchdog's maintained multi-year ratio history.

Verified sales + uniformity

State SR-1A usable Class 2 sales and NJ Division of Taxation Measures of Property Assessment Uniformity.

Known 2026 status

The Division of Taxation approved revaluation/reassessment list is treated as observed status, never model validation or a prediction.

Municipal fiscal pressure

Watchdog Municipal Budget Pressure v1.0 from NJ DCA property-tax and User-Friendly Budget filings.

Current certified-ratio coverage is intentionally bounded to the municipalities in Watchdog's governed ratio file. No statewide, neighboring-town or missing-value substitution is made. No owner/contact data, protected-class inference, legal conclusion, tax-bill forecast or ROBUST/Watchdog Score input is produced by this model.
