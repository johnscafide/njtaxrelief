THE TEAMS DIRECTION

More access should come with more control.

Teams is not meant to be “one Pro+ account shared by ten people.” It is being shaped as a governed organization layer with individual identities, explicit membership and clear ownership of shared work.

Every generally released Pro+ capability is intended to remain available to Teams, with organization-specific collaboration and administration layered on top.
