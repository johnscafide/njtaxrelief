    How Watchdog matches your CRM to properties

    Watchdog suggests a property when the street address in your CRM matches a New Jersey parcel in the same town (from the ZIP code or city), or when only one parcel in the state has that address. It never guesses from names or partial addresses, and every possible match waits for your OK.

    To link more contacts, give each contact a street address with its town or ZIP code in BoldTrail. Watchdog re-checks updated contacts after each sync, every 15 minutes.
