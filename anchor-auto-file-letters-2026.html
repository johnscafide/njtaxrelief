ANCHOR Auto-File Letters Are Going Out: What It Means If You’re Under 65 or 65+

New Jersey is sending ANCHOR Benefit Confirmation Letters in August 2026 to most eligible residents under 65 who are not receiving Social Security or Railroad Retirement disability benefits. The letter can mean the State has already prepared your 2025 ANCHOR application for you — but the rules are very different once you are 65 or qualify through disability.

Updated August 11, 2026 · Based on current New Jersey Division of Taxation guidance for the 2025 benefit year.
The key distinction: Most eligible filers under 65 can be auto-filed for ANCHOR and receive a confirmation letter. Residents age 65 or older, and recipients of qualifying Social Security or Railroad Retirement disability benefits, are not auto-filed and must submit the combined PAS-1 application by November 2, 2026.

What does an ANCHOR Benefit Confirmation Letter mean?

For most eligible filers under 65, New Jersey is using information already available to the Division of Taxation to prepare the 2025 ANCHOR application automatically. The Benefit Confirmation Letter tells you that an ANC-1 application has been auto-filed on your behalf and gives you a chance to review the information and follow any instructions included in the notice.

Receiving the letter is not a reason to throw away your ANCHOR mail. Read it carefully and verify that the residence, filing information, and payment details are correct. If your circumstances changed or the State tells you that action is required, follow the instructions in the letter or use the official ANCHOR filing system.

If you were 64 or younger in 2025

Most eligible homeowners and renters who are not collecting qualifying disability benefits will have Form ANC-1 auto-filed and receive an ANCHOR Benefit Confirmation Letter in August 2026. If you are not auto-filed, you can still submit the 2025 ANCHOR application by the November 2, 2026 deadline.

If you were 65 or older in 2025

Do not rely on an ANCHOR auto-file letter. The State says it will not automatically file PAS-1 for residents age 65+ or qualifying disability recipients. You must file PAS-1 yourself to be considered for ANCHOR, Senior Freeze, and Stay NJ.

Homeowners 65+ also have a higher 2025 ANCHOR amount

Age matters for the 2025 ANCHOR benefit calculation. Homeowners who were 65 or older by December 31, 2025 receive an additional $250: up to $1,750 for 2025 NJ gross income of $150,000 or less, or $1,250 for income from $150,001 through $250,000. Homeowners age 64 or younger receive up to $1,500 or $1,000 at those same income levels. Renters age 65+ can receive $700, compared with $450 for renters age 64 or younger, when income is $150,000 or less.

What homeowners under 65 should do now

  • Watch your mail for the ANCHOR Benefit Confirmation Letter during August 2026.
  • Review the letter rather than assuming no action is needed.
  • If you do not receive an auto-file letter, use the official ANCHOR filing process once available for you.
  • Remember that 2025 ANCHOR payments begin on a rolling basis starting September 15 and are generally issued within about 90 days after filing, unless more information is needed.
  • The filing deadline for the 2025 application is November 2, 2026.

What homeowners 65+ should do now

If you were age 65 or older during 2025, file Form PAS-1. That single application is used to determine eligibility for ANCHOR, Senior Freeze, and Stay NJ. The State will coordinate the programs and determine how much you qualify to receive from each one.

For Stay NJ, the newest FY2027 budget rules cap 2025 income at $200,000, with annualized maximum benefits of $6,500 for income up to $100,000, $5,000 for income from $100,000.01 to $150,000, and $4,000 for income from $150,000.01 to $200,000. The 2025 Stay NJ benefit is scheduled to be paid in February and May 2027, while the remaining 2024 Stay NJ installments are scheduled for August and November 2026.

Senior Freeze checks are already moving

The first 2025 Senior Freeze checks were mailed July 15, 2026. Additional rounds are scheduled according to when the PAS-1 application is filed. The program reimburses eligible seniors and qualifying disabled residents for property-tax increases above their established base year.

One last warning about scams

Property tax relief notices can attract phishing attempts. Do not treat an unsolicited text message or email as proof that it came from New Jersey. When in doubt, use the official State of New Jersey Division of Taxation website or published state contact information to verify a notice.
