What this data is and is not

Source. NJ Office of GIS statewide parcels joined to the Division of Taxation MOD-IV assessment records. Refreshed once a year, usually late spring.

Owner names are blank. Redacted under Daniel's Law. That means the parcel cannot prove your contact is the owner. Owner and renter status here is inference from property class, unit count, and unit numbers, plus anything your contact file already told us. Always reviewable and overridable per row.

The tax figure is last year's billed amount, not a current-year estimate. Great for a ballpark, not for quoting exact numbers to a client.

Not every address matches. Condos, newly subdivided lots, and multi-lot tax bills are the usual misses. Those land in Needs review.
