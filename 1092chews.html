What Is a Stormwater Detention Basin?

It is a shallow grassed depression designed to collect rainwater runoff during storms. Instead of water rushing into streets all at once, it pools temporarily and slowly filters into the soil over 24 to 72 hours. Between storms it just looks like a low grassy area -- it does not hold permanent standing water when maintained correctly.

The basin here is on the neighboring Lot 25. You cannot see it from this property and it does not affect your yard or daily use. What it does affect is a small annual cost share split among 5 lots.

Easement 1 -- Estates Lane (Shared Private Driveway)

Estates Lane is the only vehicle access from Chews Landing Rd to this property and two other lots behind it. All three lots share maintenance costs equally. The owner of Lot 26.01 administers the lane. If any owner refuses their share, legal action and liens are possible. There is no other way in or out by vehicle.

Easement 2 -- Shared Stormwater Basin (5 Lots, Equal Split)

All five lots in the subdivision share basin maintenance, administered by the owner of Lot 25. Costs split equally regardless of lot size. The 2008 engineering estimate was $2,075 total per year -- roughly $415 per lot. Actual costs may vary.

If owners fail to maintain the basin, Gloucester Township can step in, perform the work, and bill owners directly or assess it as a property tax. Non-payment can result in a lien. This obligation must appear in the deed you receive at closing.
