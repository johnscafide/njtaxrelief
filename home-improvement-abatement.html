The Rules

The 30-Day Window

Strict Filing Rule

You must file **Form E/A-1** with your local Tax Assessor within **30 days** of the project's completion. If you miss this window, you lose the 5-year savings permanently.

Qualifying Projects

  • Finished Basements
  • Kitchen & Bath Modernization
  • New Decks or Porches
  • Additions (up to 30% of house size)
