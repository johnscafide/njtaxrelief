Does Watchdog Move renew automatically?

No. It is a one-time $29 purchase for 90 days. Another term only starts if you return and deliberately purchase another 90 days.

What happens after 90 days?

The Move entitlement ends automatically. Your account remains available at the free Watchdog level, including the data and account state that the free plan normally keeps.

Why is there a 10-property limit?

Move is designed for a consumer actively buying, selling or renting. The cap keeps it useful for a transaction without turning it into a low-cost professional territory or bulk-research plan.

Can my agent or other professional give me access?

Yes. Eligible professional memberships include monthly sponsored Move seats. Use the same email address they sponsored when you sign in.
