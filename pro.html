Questions

Before you choose.

Is Free a trial?

No. Free is the permanent standard account for individual, surface-level lookup and a limited saved-property workflow.

Who should choose Pro?

Choose Pro if property research is part of your recurring professional work but you generally analyze properties individually rather than processing entire territories or databases.

What does “1,000+ records” mean?

Pro+ is designed for bulk workflows that can process at least 1,000 property records in a job. Final concurrency, monthly request, export, and API limits will be stated in the service terms.

What makes Watchdog intelligence exclusive?

The underlying government records remain public. The exclusive layer is the normalization, cross-dataset relationships, longitudinal tracking, scoring, ranking, and derived property or municipal signals Watchdog creates from them.

Is API access included with Pro?

No. Direct API access begins with Pro+. Teams adds organization keys, administration, custom limits, and supported integration work.

Can Teams connect to our CRM?

Potentially, yes. Connection depends on the CRM’s available API, your account permissions, the intended fields, volume, and permitted use. Teams includes integration scoping and implementation options.

Can this data be used for credit or tenant decisions?

No. Watchdog is not a consumer reporting agency. The data may not be used for tenant screening, employment, insurance eligibility, credit decisions, or another purpose governed by consumer-reporting law.

Does Watchdog expose owner names?

No. Owner names are redacted at the public-data source under Daniel’s Law and are not reconstructed or added back.
