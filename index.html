Program change, now law Updated June 30, 2026

Stay NJ survived the budget, with a lower income limit

New Jersey passed its FY2027 budget and Stay NJ was not cut the way the Governor first proposed. The top credit stays at $6,500. But the income limit dropped from $500,000 to about $200,000, and the credit is now capped by income:

Income $100,000 or lessup to $6,500
$100,000 to $150,000up to $5,000
$150,000 to $200,000up to $4,000
Over $200,000not eligible
Eligibility uses NJ gross income, which is not the same as your federal AGI, so check your NJ number before you assume you're out. Exact thresholds will be confirmed by the NJ Division of Taxation. The Senior Freeze was not cut. Official Stay NJ page

One form covers all three programs

If you're 65 or older, you don't file three applications. The PAS-1 covers ANCHOR, Stay NJ, and the Senior Freeze at once.

  1. Gather your numbers. Your annual property tax bill and your NJ gross income for the benefit year. That's most of what the form asks for.

  2. File the PAS-1 by November 2, 2026. File online at propertytaxrelief.nj.gov or on paper. You file it once a year.

  3. The state calculates all three benefits. You don't pick a program or do any math. The Division of Taxation determines what you get from that one form.
