It's official: Stay NJ survived the budget, with a lower income limit

New Jersey passed its FY2027 state budget, and Stay NJ was not cut the way the Governor first proposed. The full $6,500 maximum stays in place. But the income limit to qualify dropped sharply, from $500,000 down to about $200,000, and the benefit is now tiered by income. Here is what changed.

Before (through FY2026)
Cuts an eligible senior's property tax bill in half, up to $6,500.
Open to homeowners 65 and older with income up to $500,000.
Now law (FY2027 budget)
Top benefit stays at $6,500, the income limit is now about $200,000, and the amount is tiered:
Income $100,000 or lessup to $6,500
$100,000 to $150,000up to $5,000
$150,000 to $200,000up to $4,000
Over $200,000not eligible
Earn over $200,000 and you no longer qualify for Stay NJ. Eligibility uses NJ gross income, which is not the same as your federal AGI, so check your NJ number before you assume you're out. The exact income thresholds will be confirmed by the NJ Division of Taxation. The Senior Freeze program was not cut.

The main relief programs

Most NJ residents qualify for at least one of these. You have to apply to get them.

ANCHOR

A direct payment for NJ homeowners and renters, with no age requirement. Up to $1,500 for homeowners, up to $700 for renters.

Stay NJ · new $200K income limit

For homeowners 65 and older. Cuts an eligible senior's property tax bill in half, up to $6,500.

Senior Freeze (PTR)

For seniors and disabled homeowners. Reimburses the increase in your property taxes above your base year.

Veterans benefits
