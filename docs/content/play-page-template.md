# Watchdog public Play page template

Every indexable Play must contain:
1. One search intent and one professional job-to-be-done.
2. Unique title, description, H1 and self-canonical.
3. A step-by-step source-first workflow.
4. A worked New Jersey example that is not noun-swapped boilerplate.
5. A prominent “what the data cannot tell you” section.
6. Official-source links appropriate to the workflow.
7. At least two genuine FAQs with FAQPage structured data.
8. WebPage + BreadcrumbList structured data.
9. CTA to the gated product; never expose gated tooling just to create an SEO page.
10. No claims about a person’s motivation, protected traits, creditworthiness, distress or transaction intent from property records.

Do not index a Play that cannot satisfy this template with unique value.
