2026 Critical Filing Windows

January 15 Burlington/Gloucester Appeals

Final day to file traditional tax appeals in BurlCo and GloucCo.

April 1 Camden County Appeals

Deadline for Camden County and most of NJ. Note: Towns under revaluation may extend to May 1.

July 15 Senior Freeze Payments Start

First wave of checks for residents who filed their PAS-1 early (before May 1).

September ANCHOR Rebate Distribution

State begins issuing ANCHOR benefits to eligible homeowners and renters.

November 2 PAS-1 Final Deadline

The absolute last day to apply for 2026 ANCHOR, Senior Freeze, and Stay NJ.
