Planning to Retire or Age in Place in NJ? Read This Before You Apply.

The Stay NJ credit is real money, but it caps out at $6,500 a year and follows your current tax bill. For a lot of seniors, the bigger savings come from the move itself: a lower-tax South Jersey town, a smaller single-story home, or both can cut your yearly costs well beyond what any state credit covers. And you keep your credit eligibility at the new place.

John and Heather help seniors run both numbers side by side, what you'd save by staying versus what you'd save by moving, before you make any decision. No pressure either way.
