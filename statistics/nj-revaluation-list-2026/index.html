Why Watchdog does not publish a “likely next” score here

Approval is evidence

The state list identifies districts recognized for implementation. That is stronger than converting market movement, Director's Ratio drift or uniformity measures into a speculative forecast.

Revaluation is not a tax increase

A revaluation changes assessed values toward current market levels. The tax levy and resulting rate still determine how the total burden is distributed. A higher assessment does not mechanically mean the same percentage increase in tax.

Watch the evidence, not a rumor

Use the municipality report and current official notices for local timing. Watchdog can surface pressure and change context elsewhere without presenting those signals as an approved revaluation.

Source and methodology

Primary source: New Jersey Division of Taxation, Property Administration, 2026 Approved Revaluations and Reassessments, updated May 13, 2026. View the official PDF.

The page loads Watchdog's maintained /property/revaluation-reassessment-2026.json and joins each approved district code to /towns/town-manifest.json, the same municipal manifest used across Watchdog town reports. If either governed file is unavailable, the table fails closed rather than inventing municipality names or approval status.
