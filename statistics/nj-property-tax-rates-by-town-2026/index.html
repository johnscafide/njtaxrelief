What this ranking means, and what it does not

Use General Tax Rate for the bill

New Jersey defines the General Tax Rate as the multiplier used to determine the amount of tax levied on a property. It is applied to taxable assessed value.

Do not rank towns by rate alone

A high General Tax Rate does not automatically mean a homeowner has a higher effective burden than someone in another municipality. Assessment levels differ, especially across towns at different points in their revaluation cycle.

Effective Tax Rate is different

The state says the Effective Tax Rate is a statistical comparison measure and is not the rate used to compute an individual tax bill. Watchdog keeps those concepts separate.
